Google SEO or Baidu SEO?

Google and Baidu are separate search ecosystems, with different crawling, indexing, language and technical requirements. Businesses selling to international buyers need Google and international SEO first. Companies targeting customers inside mainland China also need Baidu, assessed separately with Simplified Chinese keywords and hosting that performs well in China.

Hurfi’s SEO work is aimed at overseas buyers searching in Google. We do not offer Baidu SEO, and we will tell you if your market needs it.

Our SEO process

  1. Website SEO audit. Technical condition, content, indexability, structure and existing search performance.
  2. Competitor analysis. How relevant competitors target keywords, structure content and win visibility.
  3. Keyword research and mapping. Keywords chosen by intent, relevance, target country and competition, each mapped to the best page.

Questions about SEO in Guangzhou

What does an SEO agency in Guangzhou provide?

Keyword research, technical SEO, on-page optimisation, content strategy and link building to improve visibility in organic search, plus international, e-commerce and local SEO depending on your customers and objectives.

How much do SEO services cost in Guangzhou?

It depends on website size, competition, target countries, keywords and how much ongoing optimisation is needed. International SEO and large e-commerce sites need more work than small business websites. We prepare a proposal based on your requirements.

How long does SEO take to show results?

There is no fixed timeline. It depends on your site’s authority, technical condition, competition, content and the work done. Some improvements show earlier; competitive international keywords can take considerably longer. Regular monitoring shows progress.

Can SEO help Guangzhou manufacturers attract international buyers?

Yes. Product keyword research, technical content, category optimisation and country targeting improve visibility for relevant product and industry searches abroad, attracting visitors who may become distributors, procurement contacts or customers.

What is the difference between Google SEO and Baidu SEO?

Google SEO targets Google’s search ecosystem; Baidu SEO targets China’s domestic search. They differ in crawling, indexing, language and technical requirements. Businesses targeting international buyers need Google SEO, which is what Hurfi provides; companies targeting mainland Chinese customers may also need Baidu optimisation, which we do not offer.

Does Hurfi provide international SEO for Chinese exporters?

Yes. Our international SEO covers country-specific keyword research, international site structure, content optimisation, technical SEO and multilingual planning, based on your export markets, products and current website.

What is included in a technical SEO audit?

Crawling, indexing, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, canonical URLs, redirects, broken links, mobile usability, Core Web Vitals and structured data. The findings show which technical issues affect search engine access and performance.

Can SEO improve e-commerce website visibility?

Yes. E-commerce SEO improves how product and category pages are structured and optimised, through keyword research, category optimisation, technical SEO, internal linking, product descriptions and structured data.

No. We work with businesses across Guangzhou, including Tianhe, Huangpu, Panyu, Baiyun and Nansha, remotely through online meetings, WeChat and email. Our offices are in Xi’an, China, and Bogura, Bangladesh.
