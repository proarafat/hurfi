Questions

Questions about website development in Shenzhen

What does a website development agency in Shenzhen do?

It helps businesses plan, design and build professional websites that support their goals: attracting customers, showcasing products and generating enquiries. Services usually include website planning, UI/UX design, custom development, e-commerce, SEO-friendly structure, integrations and ongoing improvements.

How much does website development cost in Shenzhen?

It depends on the size of the site, design requirements, features, integrations, content and technology. A simple business website takes less work than a custom B2B platform, an e-commerce site or a manufacturing website with advanced functionality. We quote after a short discussion of your project.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on complexity, number of pages, design, content preparation and features. A basic business website may take a few weeks; larger B2B, e-commerce or custom projects need more planning and development time.

What types of websites can you build for Shenzhen companies?

Corporate websites, B2B websites, manufacturing websites, e-commerce stores, landing pages and custom web applications. The right solution depends on your goals, customers and requirements.

Can you build websites for Shenzhen exporters and B2B companies?

Yes. B2B websites for exporters focus on attracting business customers, with product catalogues, company profiles, enquiry forms, technical information and SEO-friendly pages that help international buyers find you.

What should a manufacturing website include?

Clear product pages, company information, manufacturing capabilities, industry applications, technical details, certifications, case studies and enquiry options. These help buyers understand your expertise and decide whether to start a conversation.

What is the difference between a normal website and a B2B website?

A normal website mainly gives general business information. A B2B website is built to attract business buyers and generate enquiries, with detailed product pages, industry-specific content, lead forms and information that supports longer purchasing decisions.

Do you build SEO-friendly websites?

Yes. We build proper site structures, optimised page layouts, fast performance, mobile responsiveness and search-friendly content organisation, which gives future SEO work a stronger foundation.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. A redesign can update the design, user experience, performance, mobile compatibility, structure and conversion elements, so you present your products and services more effectively. Sometimes targeted fixes are enough, and we will say so.

What technologies do you use for website development?

It depends on the project. Options include WordPress, Shopify, React, Next.js, Node.js, PHP and custom frameworks; we choose based on scalability, functionality, maintenance and your business objectives.

Can you create multilingual websites for international customers?

Yes. A properly structured multilingual website, with correct language tags for search engines, lets customers read about your products in their own language and supports visibility in each market.

Does Hurfi have an office in Shenzhen?

No. We work with Shenzhen businesses remotely over WeChat, email and video calls. Our offices are in Xi’an, China, and Bogura, Bangladesh.
