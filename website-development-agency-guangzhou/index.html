Our website development services in Guangzhou

A manufacturer with a large catalogue needs a different structure from an exporter with a small range or a company selling online, so we build around each business.

B2B websites for manufacturers

Purchasing managers, distributors and technical buyers should understand what you supply without searching through unrelated pages. We structure B2B websites around:

  • Company introduction and manufacturing capabilities
  • Product categories and product pages
  • Technical specifications and downloadable datasheets
  • Industries and applications served
  • Factory facilities and quality control
  • OEM/ODM capabilities, where relevant
  • Request-for-quotation forms
  • Business contact information

Corporate websites

A corporate website communicates your identity, services, capabilities and business information, with clear navigation and a structure that can grow. The content reflects your actual company, not generic claims that could describe any manufacturer.

Custom website development

Some businesses need more than a template: tailored page structures, custom functionality, third-party integrations and business-specific workflows. Depending on the project we work with React, Next.js, Node.js and PHP, and we choose the simplest approach that does the job.

WordPress and Shopify development

WordPress suits corporate websites, content-led sites, product catalogues and landing pages. Shopify suits businesses that sell online and manage a shop. The right platform depends on how you sell, manage products and handle enquiries.

E-commerce websites

  • Product category organisation
  • Product pages with images and descriptions
  • Shopping cart and checkout
  • Payment integration, where supported
  • Mobile-friendly layouts
  • Product and inventory management
  • Enquiry options

For international sales, plan shipping information, payment availability, currency and language from the start.

Export and foreign-trade websites

A foreign-trade website speaks to people who may never visit your factory or meet your sales team. It can include:

  • English company and product pages
  • Manufacturing capability information
  • Specifications and applications
  • MOQ and sample information you supply
  • Production and delivery information
  • Export markets served
  • Relevant certifications and documents
  • RFQ forms for international buyers

Your website should clearly separate products you make in-house from products sourced through partners. Accurate information helps buyers evaluate you.

Website features that turn visitors into enquiries

Product catalogue and specification pages

Give each main product family its own page with descriptions, specifications, materials, applications and documents. Put key specifications in the page text, not only in images or PDFs.

Request-for-quotation forms

Ask only for what your sales team needs: product or part name, quantity, specifications, target delivery date, target market, contact details and drawings. A complicated form discourages buyers.

CRM integration

Website forms can feed a CRM such as HubSpot or Zoho, depending on the project, so enquiries land straight in your sales workflow.

Responsive design

Buyers visit on desktops, tablets and phones; layouts adapt so they can browse products and send enquiries easily.

Speed and technical foundations

We optimise images, page structure and mobile performance, and check the site from the markets where your buyers are.
