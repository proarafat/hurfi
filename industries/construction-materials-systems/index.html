Construction materials in 2025: the official figures

China’s national statistics show a domestic market under pressure, which is pushing manufacturers to export.

  • Cement output was 1.69 billion tonnes in 2025, down 6.9%, and non-metallic mineral products value added fell 0.6%.[1]
  • Construction value added was 8,642.5 billion yuan, down 1.1%, and real-estate development investment fell 17.2% to 8,278.8 billion yuan.[1]
  • Steel product exports rose 7.5% to 119.02 million tonnes, while their value fell 0.7% to 591.1 billion yuan.[1]
  • In Foshan, building materials value added fell 14.3%.[2]
  • Excavator exports rose 16.1% to 116,739 units, according to the China Construction Machinery Association.[3]

Export volumes are rising while prices fall. Buyers abroad have more choice, and they choose suppliers who can prove product performance and deliver to site reliably.

What overseas buyers compare in construction materials

Construction buyers compare suppliers within system groups and against project specifications:

Facades, windows and glazing

Thermal, acoustic, wind-load and fire performance with test evidence.

Steel structures and prefabricated buildings

Design support, fabrication standards, coating and erection guidance.

Boards, panels and insulation

Fire classification, thermal values and moisture resistance.

Tiles, stone and surfaces

Slip resistance, dimensions, finishes and batch consistency.

Sanitary ware, fittings and hardware

Approvals for water contact, durability and finish.

Roofing and waterproofing

Membrane performance, installation systems and warranties.

Standards, rules and documents buyers ask about

These references come up repeatedly with buyers in Europe and markets that follow European rules:

  • The EU Construction Products Regulation was revised in 2024. The Commission describes declarations of performance and conformity, CE marking, notified bodies and a digital product passport, and obligations for manufacturers, importers, distributors and online sellers.[4]
  • By affixing the CE marking, a manufacturer declares that a product meets all EU legal requirements for CE marking; this applies to products made outside the EU too.[5]
  • The revised buildings directive aims to raise the EU renovation rate, currently about 1% a year, which drives demand for insulation, windows and facade systems.[6]
  • Google’s guidance on multi-regional and multilingual sites explains how to signal language and country versions, which matters when you serve several export markets.[7]

Publish declarations of performance and test reports per product, with the standard and test body. For markets outside the EU, say which local approvals you hold.

Questions overseas buyers ask construction materials suppliers

What buyers ask first, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

What performance can you prove?

Test reports for fire, thermal, structural or acoustic properties, per product.

Do you have CE marking or local approvals?

Declarations and certificates per product and market.

Can you meet our project schedule?

Production and shipping times, and how you sequence deliveries.

Do you provide design and installation support?

Drawings, BIM files, installation guides and site support.

Where have your products been used?

Project references by country and building type.

How is it packed and protected in transit?

Packing method and container loading for heavy or fragile items.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for construction materials manufacturers

Construction products are specified by architects and engineers and bought by contractors and distributors. Our usual order:

  1. Organise the site by system and building type, with clear routes for specifiers, contractors and distributors.
  2. Give each product a page with performance data, dimensions, finishes and downloadable technical files.
  3. Publish a declarations and approvals page by product and market.
  4. Add project references with photos, location, building type and products used.
  5. Offer BIM or CAD files and installation guides where you have them.
  6. Build market pages for the regions you serve, covering approvals and logistics.
  7. Track which products and markets bring RFQs, and expand those.

Questions

Questions from construction materials manufacturers

Is CE marking needed for our products in Europe?

For many construction products covered by harmonised standards it is. Your notified body or consultant can confirm what applies.

Should we publish prices?

Usually not for project products. Publish specifications and invite RFQs with project details.

Which export markets should we target?

Those where your approvals, logistics and price fit. Market pages should answer local questions.

Can you help with BIM content?

We can host and present BIM and CAD files properly; producing them needs your engineering team or a specialist.

Can we name projects we supplied?

Yes, with permission from the client or contractor.

Will you guarantee project wins?

No. We improve how you are found and assessed and report actual inquiries.

Is Hurfi based in Foshan?

No. Hurfi’s offices are in Xi’an and Bogura; we work with manufacturers anywhere in China remotely.
