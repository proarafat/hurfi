Export marketing for Chinese industrial machinery and CNC manufacturers

Machinery buyers abroad want to see the machine working, read the specification and know who will install and service it. Hurfi helps Chinese machinery and CNC manufacturers show all three clearly.

Book a Consultation →

What overseas buyers check before they contact a machinery and CNC supplier

  • Machine specifications: travels, spindle speed and power, positioning accuracy and repeatability, table load, and the measurement standard used.
  • The CNC controller and key components (for example the brand of controller, spindle, guideways), stated accurately.
  • Safety and conformity: CE marking or other market requirements, with the declaration of conformity available.
  • Video of the machine cutting real parts, with the material and cycle time shown.
  • Installation, training, spare parts and remote service options abroad.

What the website and search work should include

  • Machine pages with specification tables in HTML and a short video for each model.
  • Pages by application or industry — for example mould making or automotive parts — linking to suitable machines.
  • A dealer page that explains territory, stock, training and support.
  • Search work on machine-type and specification queries, plus questions buyers ask about service and parts.
  • LinkedIn content from factory acceptance tests and customer installations, with permission.

Gaps we often see

  • Specifications in a catalog PDF only.
  • Stock photos or renderings instead of real machines.
  • No clear answer about who installs and services the machine outside China.

Search topics worth their own page

Overseas machinery and CNC buyers rarely search for a supplier by company type alone. They search for the product, the specification or the problem. These are the pages we usually plan first:

  • Machine pages with working envelope, spindle, axis, tolerance and controller details in HTML tables.
  • Video pages that show the machine cutting real parts, with the material and cycle time named.
  • Pages for the industries and parts you machine for, so buyers searching by application find a relevant machine.
  • Installation, training and service pages that answer the main worry of buying a machine from abroad.
  • Spare-parts and consumables pages that make reordering easy after the sale.
  • Retrofit, automation and option pages: loaders, probes, tool changers and what they add.
