Safety and testing in 2025: the official figures

China’s statistics show a large and growing testing and certification system, and city bulletins show strong instrument output.

  • China had 874 national quality inspection and testing centres at the end of 2025 and 1,216 product, system and service certification bodies, which have certified 1.17 million firms.[1]
  • 4,929 national standards were set or revised in 2025, 2,481 of them new.[1]
  • Nanjing’s instruments and meters grew 12.1%, and Suzhou’s medical instruments and instrumentation 9.6%.[2][3]
  • Taizhou in Zhejiang made 12.3525 million industrial instruments (+2.2%).[4]
  • The Commission issued the sixth edition of the ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU guidelines in January 2026.[5]

In this sector, the certificate is the product as far as many buyers are concerned. A site that presents certificates clearly, with scope and verification, earns trust quickly.

Standards, rules and documents buyers ask about

Certification rules are strict and buyers check them:

  • PPE sold in the EU falls under Regulation (EU) 2016/425, with notified bodies listed in the NANDO system. The Commission warns that voluntary certificates are not a recognised means of proving compliance and have no value in market surveillance checks.[6]
  • The ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU covers equipment and protective systems for potentially explosive atmospheres, such as offshore platforms, petrochemical plants, mines and flour mills.[5]
  • ISO/IEC 17025 lets testing and calibration laboratories show they operate competently and generate valid results, and helps their reports be accepted across countries.[7]
  • By affixing the CE marking, a manufacturer declares that the product meets all legal requirements for it.[8]

Show the notified body, certificate number, standard and model scope for each certificate, and link to the verification where possible. Never present a voluntary certificate as EU certification.

Safety buyers are procurement, HSE and quality professionals who check every claim. Our usual order:

Questions

Questions from industrial safety and testing manufacturers

How should we show certificates?

With certifying body, certificate number, standard, models covered and validity, and a link to verify where possible.

Are voluntary certificates useful?

Not as proof of EU compliance; the Commission says they have no value in market surveillance checks. Do not present them as such.

Can we target searches by standard number?

Yes. Standard numbers are specific searches used by serious buyers.

Should we sell through distributors?

Often yes for PPE and instruments. The site should support both distributors and direct buyers.

Can you write technical safety content?

Yes, from your technical team’s input; they approve all claims.
