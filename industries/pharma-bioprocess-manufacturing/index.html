Export marketing for Chinese pharma and bioprocess equipment manufacturers

Pharmaceutical and biotech buyers abroad check documentation and validation support before they consider a new equipment supplier. Hurfi helps Chinese manufacturers present that support accurately in English.

Book a Consultation →

What overseas buyers check before they contact a pharma and bioprocess equipment supplier

  • Materials and finish: for example 316L stainless steel, surface roughness values and material certificates.
  • Documentation packages: design documents, factory and site acceptance test procedures (FAT/SAT), and support for the customer’s qualification (IQ/OQ).
  • Design for cleaning and sterilisation, stated as engineering facts rather than compliance promises.
  • Control system and software features relevant to data integrity, where your team has documented them.
  • Installation, training, spare parts and service outside China.

What the website and search work should include

  • Equipment pages with specifications, P&ID-style overviews and documentation lists.
  • A validation-support page that says exactly which documents you supply.
  • Separate pages for standard equipment and custom projects.
  • Search work on equipment-type and process-step queries.
  • All regulatory wording reviewed by your quality team before publication.

Gaps we often see

  • “GMP certified equipment” used as a blanket claim; GMP compliance belongs to the user’s facility and process.
  • No list of the documentation actually supplied.
  • No information on after-sales service abroad.

Search topics worth their own page

Overseas pharma and bioprocess equipment buyers rarely search for a supplier by company type alone. They search for the product, the specification or the problem. These are the pages we usually plan first:

  • Equipment pages that state materials of construction, surface finish, capacity and the cleaning methods supported.
  • Documentation pages listing what you supply for qualification — design documents, FAT and SAT protocols, material certificates.
  • Compliance content that explains which GMP expectations your equipment is designed to support, without claiming certification you do not hold.
  • Process pages for fermentation, filtration, filling or packaging lines, with the products used at each step.
  • Service and validation-support pages: who helps with installation and qualification abroad.
  • Spare-parts and consumables pages, including single-use components where relevant.
