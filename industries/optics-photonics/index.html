Export marketing for Chinese optics and photonics manufacturers

Optical engineers abroad judge a supplier on tolerances, coatings and test data. Hurfi helps Chinese optics and photonics manufacturers publish that detail in a form engineers can search and compare.

Book a Consultation →

What overseas buyers check before they contact a optics and photonics supplier

  • Catalog and custom capability: materials, size ranges, surface quality, flatness and centration tolerances.
  • Coatings: wavelength ranges, reflectance or transmission curves, and laser damage threshold data with test conditions.
  • Metrology: which inspection equipment is used (for example interferometers) and what test reports ship with the parts.
  • For laser products: output specifications and the laser safety classification under IEC 60825-1.
  • Prototype and volume lead times, and how drawings and specifications are exchanged.

What the website and search work should include

  • Separate sections for catalog parts and custom manufacturing.
  • Coating and capability pages with real curves and data.
  • An RFQ path that accepts drawings and states the tolerances you can meet.
  • Search work on component-type, wavelength and tolerance queries.
  • LinkedIn content showing metrology and production steps.

Gaps we often see

  • Capability tables with no tolerances or test conditions.
  • Coating curves shown as small images without data.
  • Custom and catalog products mixed together, which confuses both buyer types.

Search topics worth their own page

Overseas optics and photonics buyers rarely search for a supplier by company type alone. They search for the product, the specification or the problem. These are the pages we usually plan first:

  • Component pages — lenses, filters, fibres, lasers, modules — with wavelength, tolerance and coating data in tables.
  • Custom-optics pages that explain what drawings and specifications you need and how prototypes are made.
  • Metrology pages that show how you measure and report quality, with sample test reports.
  • Application pages for imaging, sensing, telecom, medical and industrial lasers, each with relevant parts.
  • Export-control and compliance information where it applies to your products.
  • Packaging and handling information for delicate optics shipped long distances.
