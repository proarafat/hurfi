Export marketing for Chinese medical device and diagnostics manufacturers

Distributors and OEM partners abroad check quality systems and regulatory status before anything else. Hurfi helps Chinese medical device manufacturers present that information accurately in English, without overstating approvals.

Book a Consultation →

What overseas buyers check before they contact a medical device and diagnostics supplier

  • The quality management system certificate (for example ISO 13485), its certification body and its scope.
  • Regulatory status per market, stated exactly: CE marking under the EU MDR or IVDR, FDA registration or clearance, or other approvals — and only for the models they cover.
  • Instructions for use, labelling and UDI information where applicable.
  • What the manufacturer supports for distributors: registration documents, technical files, training and complaint handling.
  • OEM and private-label options, if you offer them, with the responsibilities on each side.

What the website and search work should include

  • Product pages that separate approved markets from markets where a product is not yet registered.
  • A regulatory and quality page with certificates, scope and validity dates.
  • A distributor page that explains the support package without promising approvals you do not control.
  • Search work on product-category and distributor-focused queries, avoiding medical claims.
  • Wording reviewed by your regulatory team before publication; Hurfi does not give regulatory advice.

Gaps we often see

  • “FDA approved” or “CE certified” used loosely for a whole range when it applies to specific models or registrations.
  • Clinical or performance claims without the study or data behind them.
  • No information about vigilance and complaint handling, which serious distributors ask about.

Search topics worth their own page

Overseas medical device and diagnostics buyers rarely search for a supplier by company type alone. They search for the product, the specification or the problem. These are the pages we usually plan first:

  • Product pages that state the intended use and the regulatory status in each target market, without claims your clearance does not cover.
  • Quality-system pages showing ISO 13485 scope and the markets where your devices are registered or CE-marked.
  • OEM and private-label pages for distributors who want to sell under their own brand, with the documentation you can supply.
  • Content for clinical buyers that explains workflow, training and maintenance in plain terms.
  • Distributor pages listing what partners receive: registration support, training, demo units and service.
  • Clear separation between professional and consumer products, so each audience finds the right information.
