Standing out online when your whole city makes the same product

In many Chinese cities, hundreds of factories make the same kind of product. A buyer searching for pumps, printed fabric or wigs will find dozens of suppliers that look alike. This guide explains how to make your website the one they contact.

The cluster problem

Official bulletins show how concentrated some industries are. In 2025 Taizhou (Zhejiang) made 64.0521 million pumps and 202.2984 million pairs of eyeglasses, and had 9,418 exporting firms. Shaoxing made 26.349 billion metres of printed and dyed cloth. Xuchang exported 17.2 billion yuan of hair products. In places like these, “manufacturer of high-quality products” describes everyone.

Find your real difference

Ask your sales team what buyers chose you for last year. The answers are usually specific:

  • A product range or size others do not make.
  • A material, finish or performance level.
  • Certifications for a demanding market.
  • Speed on samples or small orders.
  • Engineering support or customisation.

Prove it with specifics

Replace adjectives with facts: tolerances, test results, certificates, capacity, lead times, the number of models. Specific numbers that you can support are more convincing than any slogan.

Go deeper on products than anyone else

Most cluster websites have thin product pages: a photo and a few lines. Give each product family a real page with specifications in text, applications, options and downloadable datasheets. Where it fits, Product structured data can help describe products to Google.

Show that you are the factory

Buyers in clusters often cannot tell factories from traders. Show your own production lines, testing, warehouse and team, with your own photos and captions. Give your address and say what you make in-house and what you buy in.

Answer questions before they are asked

  • MOQ and sample terms.
  • Lead times for standard and custom products.
  • Certifications by market.
  • Shipping ports and terms.
  • OEM and private-label options.

Write for buyers, not for keywords

Google’s guidance on helpful, reliable, people-first content asks whether content provides original information and substantial value compared with other pages. In a cluster, original content means your own products, data and photos — not text copied from a neighbour’s site or a marketplace listing.

Use the cluster as context, not as your message

Being in a well-known production city can reassure buyers about supply chains and experience. Mention it, but do not make it your main message: every competitor can say the same.

A quick self-test

Open your home page and a competitor’s side by side. Cover the logos. If a buyer could not tell which is which, your site is not yet doing its job. Then ask:

  • Does our first screen say what we make that is different?
  • Can a buyer find a specification table in two clicks?
  • Are the photos clearly from our factory?
  • Do we answer MOQ, lead time and certification questions without an email?

Each “no” is a place to start.

Let specialisation lead

In a cluster, specialists often win online: the supplier that makes one kind of pump, fabric or wig very well, and says so clearly. If you have a speciality, put it first. If you make a broad range, organise it so that each family has the depth a specialist would show.

Measure what makes buyers contact you

Track which pages lead to inquiries. Over time you will see which differences buyers care about, and you can strengthen those pages first.

Questions

Common questions

Our products are similar to our neighbours’. How can we stand out?

With specifics: the products, materials, tests, certifications and services you offer that others do not, shown with your own data and photos.

Should we copy successful competitors’ websites?

No. Copied content does not help buyers or search. Learn from them, then show what is different about you.

Is it worth naming our city?

Yes, as context. But your differences, not your location, should lead.

How many products should we put online?

Start with the families that bring the best inquiries, and make those pages excellent.

Do we need our own photos?

Yes. Stock or supplier photos make you look like a trader.

Can marketplaces replace our website?

Marketplaces can bring inquiries, but your own site is where you can show everything that makes you different.

Can Hurfi guarantee we outrank competitors?

No. Nobody can. We build better pages and report honestly from your own data.
