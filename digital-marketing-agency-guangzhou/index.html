Our digital marketing services in Guangzhou

A manufacturer targeting international distributors needs a different strategy from an e-commerce brand selling to consumers. Depending on your audience and goals, we offer:

Google Ads setup and management

Reach buyers when they search for your products, services or suppliers.

  • Account setup
  • Search campaign creation
  • Keyword research
  • Competitor ad analysis
  • Ad copywriting
  • Targeting and segmentation
  • Conversion tracking
  • Budget and bidding
  • Search terms analysis
  • Ongoing monitoring

For example, a Guangzhou machinery maker might target procurement professionals searching for specific equipment in the United States, the UK or Australia.

Meta Ads: Facebook and Instagram

For eligible markets we set up and manage campaigns: audience research, ad creatives, campaign structure, retargeting, conversion tracking and reporting. Availability and targeting depend on the market and Meta’s policies; mainland China campaigns need a different platform strategy.

Social media management

Account management, monthly content plans, post design, product content, scheduling and profile optimisation. Content can show manufacturing capabilities, applications, launches and company news, on the platforms your customers actually use.

Conversion tracking: GA4 and pixels

Without tracking you cannot tell which activity produces enquiries. We set up:

  • Google Analytics 4
  • Google Tag Manager
  • Form submission tracking
  • Phone and contact click tracking
  • Google Ads conversions
  • Meta Pixel
  • Event configuration and testing

For a B2B manufacturer, a conversion is usually an enquiry, a quotation request or a consultation booking rather than a purchase.

Lead generation and funnel optimisation

Traffic is only part of the job. We plan landing pages, lead campaigns, conversion-focused page structure, contact forms, calls to action and enquiry funnels, so a buyer can go from an ad to a product page to an enquiry form.

Retargeting

Not every visitor is ready to enquire on the first visit. Meta retargeting and Google remarketing can bring back people who viewed your product pages, with appropriate consent, tracking and privacy compliance.

Email marketing setup

Account setup, enquiry follow-ups, welcome sequences and simple lead-nurturing flows, using contacts who have given permission and following data-protection rules.

Questions about digital marketing in Guangzhou

What does a digital marketing agency in Guangzhou provide?

It promotes products and services through online channels: Google Ads, social media marketing, SEO, lead generation, email marketing, conversion tracking and retargeting. The right combination depends on your audience, industry and objectives.

How much do digital marketing services cost in Guangzhou?

It depends on the services, campaign complexity, ad budget, target markets and management required. Google Ads management, social media and full digital marketing packages have different scopes, and ad spend is separate. We prepare a proposal for your requirements.

How can digital marketing help Guangzhou manufacturers?

It promotes products, reaches relevant international audiences and generates enquiries through Google Ads, SEO, product landing pages, social campaigns and conversion tracking, planned around your products, export markets and buyers.

Which digital marketing channels suit Chinese exporters?

Google Ads, international SEO, suitable social platforms, email marketing and lead-generation campaigns, chosen by target country and buyer behaviour. Domestic Chinese audiences need a separate strategy with local platforms.

Does Hurfi provide Google Ads management in Guangzhou?

Yes: keyword research, campaign creation, ad optimisation, targeting and conversion tracking for eligible international markets. Budgets and management are agreed before launch.

Can Hurfi manage Facebook and Instagram advertising?

Yes, for eligible markets: campaign planning, audience research, creatives, optimisation and conversion tracking. Availability, account eligibility and targeting depend on the market and Meta’s policies.

Is digital marketing effective for B2B manufacturing companies?

It can improve product visibility and create opportunities to reach procurement managers, distributors and partners. SEO, Google Ads, technical product pages and enquiry-focused landing pages work together to support customer acquisition.

What is included in conversion tracking?

GA4, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads conversion tracking, Meta Pixel and website events such as form submissions, phone clicks, quotation requests and consultation bookings, depending on your site access and platforms.

Can Hurfi help generate international leads for Guangzhou exporters?

We support international lead generation through advertising strategy, SEO, landing pages, conversion tracking and funnel optimisation, planned around your target countries, products and buyers. Actual enquiry volume and quality depend on demand, competition, execution and budget.
