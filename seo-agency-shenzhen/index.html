Questions

Questions about SEO in Shenzhen

What does an SEO agency in Shenzhen do?

An SEO agency helps businesses improve their visibility on Google and other search engines through keyword research, technical SEO, content optimisation, website improvements and authority building. The goal is to attract relevant visitors, reach potential customers and create more business opportunities through organic search.

Why do Shenzhen businesses need SEO services?

Shenzhen manufacturers, exporters, technology companies and B2B suppliers compete globally. SEO helps them appear in relevant searches, show their products or services, and connect with customers who are actively looking for solutions.

How can SEO help Shenzhen manufacturers get more customers?

By improving visibility for product searches, industry keywords and supplier queries. Optimised product pages, useful content and a better site structure make it easier for international buyers and partners to find your company.

What SEO services does Hurfi provide in Shenzhen?

Keyword research, on-page SEO, technical SEO, local SEO, international SEO, content optimisation, SEO audits, competitor analysis and website performance improvements, all aimed at search visibility with the right audiences.

How long does SEO take to show results?

It depends on your website’s condition, competition, industry, keyword difficulty and how much optimisation is needed. Some improvements can appear within a few months; sustainable growth needs continuous work on content and technical SEO.

How much does SEO cost for a business in Shenzhen?

It depends on website size, target markets, competition, the services needed and your goals. A small business website needs a different plan from a large manufacturer or exporter targeting several countries. We quote after reviewing your site.

Can SEO help Shenzhen exporters reach international buyers?

Yes. International SEO targets the keywords buyers use in each market, optimises multilingual content, improves site structure and creates pages that answer buyers’ questions before they make contact.

What is the difference between local SEO and international SEO?

Local SEO improves visibility for location-based searches, such as customers looking for services in Shenzhen. International SEO reaches customers in other countries. Exporters often benefit from both.

How do I choose the right SEO company in Shenzhen?

Choose one that understands your industry, goals and customers, explains its process and priorities clearly, analyses competitors, and focuses on visibility, relevant traffic and enquiries rather than promising rankings.

Can SEO improve an existing website?

Yes. An SEO audit finds technical issues, weak content, structure problems, internal-link gaps and better keywords, often without rebuilding the whole website.

What industries in Shenzhen can benefit from SEO?

Electronics manufacturers, OEM/ODM companies, technology businesses, industrial suppliers, machinery companies and exporters can all use SEO to show their expertise and reach customers searching for their products.

No. We work with Shenzhen businesses remotely over WeChat, email and video calls. Our offices are in Xi’an, China, and Bogura, Bangladesh.
