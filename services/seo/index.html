B2B SEO Services for Manufacturers & Exporters

B2B buyers search differently from everyday consumers. Procurement teams, engineers, and distributors often use technical terms, model numbers, materials, or manufacturing standards.

Our B2B SEO Services focus on these searches rather than attracting visitors who have little interest in your products.

Technical SEO Services

Technical issues can stop search engines from accessing or indexing important pages.

Our Technical SEO Services include website audits, crawling and indexing checks, XML sitemaps, canonical tags, internal linking, structured data recommendations, mobile usability, and website performance reviews.

We follow established practices explained in Google's SEO Starter Guide.

On-Page SEO Optimization

Our On-Page SEO Services improve how website pages communicate with buyers and search engines.

We research relevant keywords, map search intent, optimize titles and descriptions, improve heading structures, and strengthen page content.

For manufacturers, this includes optimizing technical product specifications, materials, applications, manufacturing capabilities, and supplier information.

Local SEO & Google Business Profile

Our Local SEO Services support businesses with genuine location-based search requirements.

Work may include Google Business Profile optimization for eligible businesses, local keyword research, business information consistency, relevant location pages, and local search performance monitoring.

We use real business locations and service coverage rather than creating misleading listings.

02

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Services

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) helps organize website content so search and answer systems can identify clear, relevant responses to user questions.

Business buyers increasingly search through detailed questions about suppliers, specifications, materials, certifications, and product applications.

Which supplier makes stainless steel valves to DIN standards?

Clear answer · Specifications · FAQ · Schema

Our AEO Services include:

  • Question-focused content research
  • Clear answers and descriptive headings
  • Useful FAQs based on customer intent
  • Structured content and relevant schema recommendations
  • Accurate company and product information
  • Internal linking between related topics
  • Content improvements for answer-oriented searches

AEO can support traditional search features and AI-assisted answers. However, no website can guarantee selection by an answer engine.

Your Website Only Appears for Your Brand Name

Frequently Asked Questions About B2B SEO

What does a B2B SEO agency do?

A B2B SEO Agency improves website visibility for searches made by business buyers. Its work may include keyword research, technical SEO, page optimization, content planning, and reporting. For manufacturers, the focus often includes product specifications, applications, sourcing requirements, and information procurement teams need before contacting suppliers.

How much do B2B SEO services cost?

Hurfi's Google SEO services start from ¥3,800 per month. Pricing depends on website complexity, target markets, competition, and the amount of optimization required. A small manufacturer and a business with hundreds of product pages may need different levels of work. Final project costs are confirmed in writing.

How long does B2B SEO take to show results?

SEO results vary according to website condition, competition, technical problems, and existing search visibility. Some changes may be reflected sooner, while competitive keywords and new content can require sustained work. Hurfi monitors available performance data rather than promising results within an unsupported timeline.

How can SEO help Chinese manufacturers find overseas buyers?

SEO helps manufacturers make relevant product and company pages discoverable through Google searches. Buyers may search using materials, applications, specifications, or supplier requirements. Optimizing pages around these needs can improve opportunities for qualified discovery, although traffic and enquiries depend on several additional business factors.

Does Hurfi provide international SEO?

Yes. Hurfi provides International SEO for Chinese manufacturers and exporters targeting overseas markets. Work can involve market-specific keyword research, language planning, page structure, technical optimization, and performance monitoring. The objective is to make the website more relevant and accessible to buyers in the countries the business serves.

Can B2B SEO help generate RFQ enquiries?

SEO can help potential buyers discover relevant product pages and reach quotation forms. Strong specifications, clear company information, and accessible contact options support the enquiry process. However, enquiry volume also depends on product demand, pricing, competition, website usability, and sales follow-up.

Does Hurfi provide Baidu SEO?

No. Hurfi's published SEO service focuses on Google and overseas buyer searches rather than domestic Baidu SEO. This approach is intended for Chinese manufacturers, exporters, and suppliers seeking visibility outside China. Businesses targeting domestic Chinese search users may require a separate Baidu-focused strategy.

Does Hurfi guarantee Google rankings?

No. Hurfi does not guarantee specific Google rankings, traffic volumes, or enquiry numbers. Search results depend on competition, website quality, algorithm changes, and other factors outside an agency's control. Hurfi focuses on relevant SEO work, transparent reporting, and identifying practical improvements.
