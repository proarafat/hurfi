International SEO for factories that export

Most of the buyers a Chinese factory wants are outside China, and they search in their own language, in Google, with their own words for the product. International SEO is the work of making sure the right pages show up for them.

  • Choosing target markets first, then the language and the wording buyers in those markets actually use.
  • A clear site structure for each market or language, with correct hreflang and canonical tags when there is more than one version.
  • Server, hosting and speed checks, because a site that loads slowly from Europe or North America loses visitors before they read anything.
  • Product and application pages written around specification-stage searches: materials, standards, tolerances, capacities and model numbers.
  • Search Console set up for your own domain, so you can see impressions and clicks by country.

What we will not do

  • Promise rankings, traffic or a number of inquiries.
  • Buy links or use tactics that put your domain at risk.
  • Offer Baidu SEO — our work is aimed at overseas buyers searching in Google.
