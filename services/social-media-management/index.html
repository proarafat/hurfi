Why LinkedIn matters for a factory

Before an overseas buyer replies to your email, they often look up the company page and the person who wrote to them. A company page with no posts, or a sales profile with no photo, raises doubts that a good price cannot fix. LinkedIn is where B2B buyers check whether a supplier is real, active and professional.

  • A company page that matches the website: same products, same certificates, same contact details.
  • Profiles for the people buyers will talk to, in clear English.
  • A posting plan built around real material: production, testing, trade shows, new products, team.
  • Every post approved by your team before it goes out; nothing is published without permission.

What we do not do

  • Buy followers or engagement.
  • Send mass connection requests or automated messages that damage your account and reputation.
  • Use stock photos in place of your own factory.
