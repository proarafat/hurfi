Why a Professional B2B Website Matters for Manufacturers

International buyers often research several suppliers before contacting one.

A professional B2B Website Design helps them evaluate products and understand a business without waiting for an initial sales conversation.

Clear answers can reduce unnecessary back-and-forth and help visitors decide whether to contact your business.

For Chinese exporters, a professional English website provides a company-owned place to share information with buyers discovered through trade shows, search engines, industry directories, and supplier platforms.

A useful manufacturing website should answer questions such as:

  1. What products does the company manufacture?
  2. What materials, specifications, or options are available?
  3. Does the factory provide OEM or ODM services?
  4. What are its manufacturing and quality control capabilities?
  5. Are relevant certificates and technical documents available?
  6. How can a buyer request pricing or samples?
  7. Who should the buyer contact for further information?

Common Website Problems We Help Manufacturers Solve

01

Overseas Buyers Cannot Find Product Information

Many factory websites display product photographs without enough technical detail.

Buyers may still need dimensions, materials, specifications, compatible applications, or available configurations.

We help organize product information into relevant categories and individual pages so visitors can review important details before making an enquiry.

02

Your Website Does Not Clearly Show Your Manufacturing Capabilities

A factory may offer CNC machining, electronics assembly, precision components, or OEM manufacturing, but its website may not explain those capabilities.

We structure relevant pages around manufacturing processes, equipment, production information, quality procedures, and supported product types using details supplied by your business.

This gives potential customers a clearer understanding of what your company can provide.

03

Visitors Leave Without Sending an Enquiry

An enquiry form hidden at the bottom of a page is easy to miss.

We review how buyers move between product pages, company information, and contact options. Clear calls to action and relevant quotation forms can make the next step easier.

Not every visitor will become a lead, but the website should not make enquiries unnecessarily difficult.

Our manufacturer website RFQ checklist explains what buyers need before they submit a quotation request.

04

Your Website Is Difficult to Use on Mobile

Buyers may open your website on a phone while travelling, attending an exhibition, or communicating with colleagues.

Mobile-friendly layouts, readable text, working navigation, and accessible contact forms help visitors use the site across different devices.

Website performance matters too. Google's Core Web Vitals guidance explains the metrics used to assess loading performance, responsiveness, and visual stability.

05

Your Website Is Not Prepared for Search Visibility

A website with unclear headings, weak page structure, and limited product information may be difficult for search engines to understand.

We consider SEO-friendly URLs, descriptive headings, internal linking, mobile usability, and technical page health during development.

These elements create a useful foundation for future SEO, although rankings depend on additional factors.

04

What Should an Export-Ready Manufacturing Website Include?

A good Export Website Development project starts with the information overseas buyers actually need.

1Specifications
2Certificates3MOQ · Lead time · Incoterms
4Request a quote
  1. 1

    Clear Product Categories and Specification Pages

    Each major product family should have a dedicated section. Individual product pages can include relevant dimensions, materials, models, applications, options, images, and downloadable datasheets.

    If a company has a large catalogue, buyers should be able to navigate between related products without returning to the homepage each time.

  2. 2

    Factory Capabilities and Quality Information

    Your website should explain what your factory can manufacture and how it manages production quality.

    Useful content may include real photographs, machinery details, testing procedures, quality control processes, and relevant certificates.

    Certification names, scopes, and expiration dates must reflect genuine company documentation.

  3. 3

    Commercial Information for International Buyers

    Depending on your business, buyers may need information about minimum order quantities, sample availability, estimated lead times, supported markets, and accepted Incoterms.

    You do not need to publish sensitive commercial information, but clear general guidance can make initial conversations more productive.

  4. 4

    Request-for-Quotation Forms

    An effective RFQ Website Development approach connects product information with an appropriate enquiry form.

    Depending on your products, useful fields may include:

    • Product or model
    • Required quantity
    • Application or project type
    • Technical drawing or file upload
    • Destination market
    • Expected delivery schedule
    • Company name and business email

    These fields should match how your sales team qualifies enquiries. Requesting too much information can discourage potential buyers.

  5. 5

    English Content Written for Buyers

    An international website needs clear product descriptions, correct technical terminology, and readable English.

    Content should explain products and capabilities accurately rather than rely on literal translations of promotional language.

    This is particularly important when engineers, purchasing managers, and distributors use your site to assess supplier suitability.

    For businesses serving multiple countries, our international SEO checklist for Chinese exporters covers language targeting, website structure, and important technical considerations.

Should You Redesign Your Existing Website or Build a New One?

A website redesign is worth considering when your current site no longer explains your products properly, does not work well on mobile, or makes enquiries difficult.

However, a complete rebuild is not always necessary.

Improve the current site

If the website's technical foundation remains suitable, improving product pages, navigation, English content, or quotation forms may be enough.

Build a new website

A new website may make more sense when the platform cannot support required functionality, the structure is difficult to maintain, or extensive changes would cost more than rebuilding.

Frequently Asked Questions About B2B Website Development

What does a B2B website development agency do?

A B2B Website Development Agency designs and builds websites for companies that sell products or services to other businesses. This may include corporate pages, product catalogues, technical documentation, and enquiry forms. For manufacturers, the goal is to help potential buyers understand capabilities, assess products, and contact the right sales team.

How much does B2B website development cost in China?

Hurfi's export-oriented B2B websites start from ¥7,999 as a one-time development price. The final quotation depends on the number of pages, product information, design requirements, integrations, and other project details. Custom features and ongoing services may carry additional costs. Hurfi confirms the scope and quotation before starting work.

How long does it take to develop a manufacturing website?

The development timeline depends on project complexity, content readiness, feedback, and required features. A small corporate website generally involves less work than a large product catalogue with custom integrations. Hurfi discusses the scope before work begins and confirms the expected delivery schedule as part of the project agreement.

What should a manufacturing company website include?

A manufacturing website should explain what the company makes, its capabilities, product specifications, quality procedures, and how buyers can request information. Useful additions include real factory photographs, relevant certifications, downloadable technical documents, and clear quotation forms. The exact structure depends on the products, target markets, and customer requirements.

Can a B2B website generate quotation enquiries from overseas buyers?

A B2B website can support quotation enquiries by making products, specifications, and contact options easy to find. Clear product pages and relevant RFQ forms help visitors submit useful information to the sales team. Results also depend on traffic quality, market demand, pricing, competitiveness, and how the business responds to enquiries.

Is WordPress suitable for B2B manufacturing websites?

WordPress can be suitable for manufacturers that need corporate pages, product catalogues, technical resources, and regular content updates. It supports different website structures and integrations. However, businesses with extensive product data or advanced ordering requirements should assess whether WordPress, Shopify, or a custom system better fits their needs.

Do Chinese exporters need an English-language website?

An English-language website can help Chinese exporters communicate with international buyers who use English during supplier research. It allows manufacturers to present product information, company capabilities, and enquiry options in a consistent format. Companies targeting specific non-English-speaking markets may also benefit from additional language versions where commercially justified.

What is the difference between a B2B website and an ecommerce website?

A B2B website often supports longer buying decisions involving product specifications, quotations, samples, and negotiated terms. An ecommerce website commonly allows visitors to place orders through an online checkout. Some businesses need both. For custom manufacturers or bulk suppliers, an enquiry-focused website may fit the sales process better than direct online purchasing.

Can Hurfi improve our existing manufacturing website?

Yes. Hurfi's website service includes reviewing existing websites and identifying whether improvements or a new build would be more appropriate. Depending on the site's condition, work may focus on product organization, mobile usability, technical health, English content, or enquiry paths. Recommendations follow an assessment of the website and business requirements.

Does Hurfi provide website development outside Shenzhen and Guangzhou?

Yes. Hurfi supports Chinese manufacturers and B2B businesses remotely, including companies in other manufacturing and export hubs. Its China contact operation is based in Xi'an, with services available beyond that city. Businesses can discuss their website requirements through Hurfi's online consultation process, email, or China contact number.
