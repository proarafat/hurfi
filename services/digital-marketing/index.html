Lead generation without inflated numbers

For a manufacturer, one qualified inquiry from the right buyer is worth more than thousands of impressions. We plan campaigns backwards from that: which buyers, in which markets, looking for which products — and which page on your site will answer them.

  • Search campaigns for buyers who are already looking for the product type or process you offer.
  • Content that answers the questions engineers and buyers ask before they write: specifications, standards, applications.
  • Paid campaigns where the numbers justify them, with spend and results shown from your own ad accounts.
  • Inquiry tracking by source, so you can see which channel produced which conversation.

What we report

Inquiries, calls and profile actions by channel, alongside spend. We do not report vanity reach as success, and we do not promise a number of leads. If a channel is not working after a fair test, we say so and move the budget.
