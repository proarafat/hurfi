What Qingdao makes, according to official data

Qingdao’s 2025 statistical bulletin, published in Qingdao Daily, gives the figures below.

  • Qingdao’s GDP was 1,756.067 billion yuan in 2025, up 5.4%, and value added of industry above designated size grew 7.9%.[1]
  • Equipment manufacturing value added grew 11.8% and high-tech manufacturing 8.4%.[1]
  • By industry, automobile manufacturing grew 25.9%, chemical raw materials and products 15.9%, special-purpose equipment 13.6%, and rail, ship, aerospace and other transport equipment 12.8%.[1]
  • Output of new-energy vehicles rose 242.3%, lithium-ion batteries 61.3%, integrated-circuit wafers 51.9% and virtual-reality devices 29.0%.[1]
  • Goods trade was 912.89 billion yuan (+0.6%), of which exports were 541.87 billion yuan (+2.7%); private firms made 72.4% of trade.[1]

Qingdao combines heavy and transport equipment with fast-growing EV, battery and electronics lines, all next to a major container port. Buyers of these products need technical detail and confidence about shipping. A website that answers both questions early shortens the qualification process.

Qingdao’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Vehicles and EV components

Vehicle and parts buyers check platforms served, quality systems and testing. Name the vehicle types, not customers you cannot name.

Chemicals and materials

Chemical buyers look for grades, specifications, safety data sheets and packaging per product, plus REACH or other registrations where held.

Rail, marine and transport equipment

Transport-equipment buyers need standards, classification-society approvals and project references with dates.

Special-purpose machinery

Machine buyers compare output, power, automation level and service abroad, one machine family at a time.

Batteries and electronics

Battery and electronics buyers ask for cell chemistry, test reports, certifications and capacity. Publish them per product line.

Industrial zones and districts in Qingdao

  • Qingdao West Coast New Area, approved by the State Council in 2014, covers all of Huangdao District: about 2,096 square kilometres of land and 5,000 square kilometres of sea.[2]
  • The approval gives it the theme of developing the marine economy, including marine science and new marine industries.[2]

Marine equipment, chemicals and port-linked manufacturing cluster on the West Coast. Buyers of these products check approvals and project references more than anything else.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Qingdao’s port handled 740 million tonnes of cargo in 2025 (+4.1%), including 530 million tonnes of foreign-trade cargo, and 32.893 million TEU of containers (+6.5%).[1]
  • Air cargo and mail fell 18.7% to 227,000 tonnes.[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Trade with Belt and Road countries was 546.00 billion yuan (+6.2%), 59.8% of the total; exports to them rose 8.9%.[1]
  • Private firms traded 660.78 billion yuan (+5.0%), 72.4% of the total, and exported 418.27 billion yuan (+7.1%).[1]

Almost 60% of Qingdao’s trade is with Belt and Road countries, and the city also has deep ties with Korea and Japan. Plan pages, certifications and languages for the markets you really serve.

Challenges Qingdao manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Qingdao.

  • Approvals hidden. Class, chemical and vehicle approvals are often missing from product pages.
  • Port advantage untold. Buyers do not know how fast you can ship unless you say so.
  • Translated Chinese pages. Direct translations rarely answer foreign buyers’ questions.
  • No reference pages. Equipment and marine buyers want proof from delivered work.

Questions overseas buyers ask Qingdao suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Which classification or certification approvals do you hold?

List approvals and certificates with scope and the products they cover.

Can you send a safety data sheet and specification?

Offer SDS and specifications per grade as downloads next to the product text.

How do you ship oversized or dangerous goods?

Explain packing, dangerous-goods handling and the ports and terms you use.

Who has used this equipment before?

Give project references you are permitted to share, with year and scope.

What support do you provide abroad?

Describe installation, training, spare parts and remote support honestly.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Qingdao manufacturers

  1. Give each product line a page with its specifications, standards and approvals in text; offer the PDF datasheet beside it.
  2. Publish a logistics page: port of loading, packing methods, dangerous-goods handling and typical transit times.
  3. Build reference pages for completed projects you are allowed to show, with dates, scope and photos.
  4. Separate pages for distributors and for end users when you sell to both.
  5. Check Google’s guidance on managing multi-regional sites before launching country or language versions.
  6. Track RFQs by product line and country so you can see which markets respond.
