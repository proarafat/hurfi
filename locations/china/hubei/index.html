Hubei’s manufacturing economy in official figures

The Hubei Provincial Bureau of Statistics’ 2025 bulletin, as republished on the Qianjiang government portal, gives the figures below.

  • Hubei’s GDP was 6,266.090 billion yuan in 2025, up 5.5%; value added of industry above designated size grew 6.9%.[1]
  • High-tech manufacturing grew 15.5% and made up 17.4% of above-designated-size value added; electronics grew 16.9%.[1]
  • Goods trade was 834.01 billion yuan (+18.2%) and exports 581.86 billion yuan (+19.6%).[1]
  • Trade with ASEAN, Hubei’s largest partner, was 175.58 billion yuan (+16.7%), and with the EU 113.66 billion yuan (+40.2%).[1]
  • Trade with Belt and Road countries was 457.61 billion yuan (+21.0%), 54.9% of the total; ports handled 2.7537 million TEU (+5.6%).[1]
  • The export delivery value of above-designated-size industry rose 9.0%; trade with other RCEP members was 265.95 billion yuan (+15.7%), 31.9% of the total.[1]

Hubei’s exporters grew fast in 2025, especially in Europe and ASEAN. Many are inland suppliers of technical products. Buyers need detailed specifications — and a clear explanation of how goods get from central China to their door. Close to a third of its trade is with other RCEP members, so Asia-Pacific buyers matter as much as Europe.

More Hubei manufacturing cities, from their 2025 bulletins

These cities do not have a full guide yet. The figures come from each city’s 2025 statistical bulletin, listed in the sources below.

Xiangyang

  • Xiangyang made 284,000 vehicles in 2025 (−4.7%), including 83,000 new-energy vehicles.[2]
  • Lithium-ion battery output rose 113.1% to 29.296 million kWh and mobile-phone output 57.5% to 5.124 million.[2]
  • Exports fell 11.6% to 30.37 billion yuan.[2]

Xiangyang’s car and parts makers face a flat vehicle market while its battery and phone lines grow fast. For both, product pages with real specifications and certifications are the starting point for new buyers. Related: Automotive & Mobility Components · Xiangyang in the China directory.

Shiyan

  • Shiyan’s GDP was 270.48 billion yuan in 2025, up 6.4%; value added of industry above designated size grew 12.3%.[3]
  • Automobile manufacturing grew 14.8%, electrical machinery 7.2% and chemicals 13.8%.[3]

In Shiyan, automobile manufacturing grew faster than industry as a whole. Vehicle and parts suppliers there should show quality systems, testing and the vehicle types they serve. Related: Automotive & Mobility Components · Shiyan in the China directory.

Website and search priorities for Hubei exporters

  1. Give each product family a page with specifications in HTML.
  2. Publish a logistics page: rail, Yangtze shipping, air and ports used.
  3. Prepare certifications for Europe before building EU market pages.
  4. Show testing, quality systems and real production photos.
  5. Plan language versions only where you have customers.
  6. Track RFQs by product and country.
