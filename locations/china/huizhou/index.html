What Huizhou makes, according to official data

The figures below come from Huizhou’s 2025 statistical bulletin, published by Huizhou Daily.

  • Huizhou’s GDP was 636.366 billion yuan in 2025, up 4.5%; industrial value added was 303.029 billion yuan (+7.8%).[1]
  • Value added of industry above designated size grew 8.6% across 5,175 firms; electronics grew 11.8%, petrochemicals, energy and new materials 5.6% and life-health manufacturing 10.8%.[1]
  • High-tech manufacturing made up 43.0% of above-designated-size value added (+11.7%), and equipment manufacturing 51.1% (+12.3%). Computers and office equipment grew 33.8%.[1]
  • Foreign trade was 428.251 billion yuan (+8.8%), of which exports were 243.095 billion yuan (+9.6%). The export delivery value of above-designated-size industry rose 6.9%.[1]
  • 954 Huizhou firms held China Compulsory Certification (CCC) for their products.[1]
  • Medical instruments and instrumentation grew 11.2% and advanced equipment manufacturing 15.9%.[1]

Huizhou’s exporters are mostly in electronics and electrical products, where buyers compare datasheets, certifications and capacity line by line. Its chemical and materials makers sell on grades and documents. In both cases the website has to do the explaining that a sales visit would otherwise do. Huizhou’s trade with Europe grew faster than its total in 2025, and European buyers are among the most demanding about documentation.

Huizhou’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Electronics and components

Component and module buyers want parameters, packages, standards and test methods per part number, in text they can search and compare.

Computers, displays and smart devices

Device buyers check specifications, certifications per market and OEM/ODM options. Keep own-brand and OEM pages separate.

Medical and life-health products

Medical buyers check registrations, quality systems and documentation exactly as held, per market.

Appliances and electrical equipment

Appliance buyers compare models, voltages, plugs and certifications per market. A model table with filters saves many emails.

Petrochemicals and new materials

Materials buyers look for grades, specifications, safety data sheets, packaging and registrations, product by product.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Huizhou’s port handled 94.17 million tonnes of cargo in 2025, up 2.8%.[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Mechanical and electrical products made up 76.7% of Huizhou’s 2025 exports, 186.36 billion yuan (+10%), Huizhou Daily reported from customs data.[2]
  • The largest export lines were household appliances (34.92 billion yuan), electronic components (34.38 billion), electrical equipment (24.48 billion) and computers and parts (23.52 billion, +23%).[2]
  • Taiwan (63.34 billion yuan), ASEAN (57.55 billion), the EU (43.18 billion, +11.3%) and Hong Kong (37.36 billion) were the main trading partners.[2]
  • General trade grew 23.3% to 210.11 billion yuan, 49.1% of trade. Foreign-invested firms made 50.3% of trade and private firms, mostly small and medium-sized, 47.5%.[2]

Huizhou trades most with Taiwan, ASEAN, the EU and Hong Kong, and EU trade grew in 2025. Sell into Europe with certifications and documentation in order; in ASEAN, show capacity, lead times and support.

Challenges Huizhou manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Huizhou.

  • Specifications in images. Buyers want searchable text they can copy into a comparison.
  • One page for every model. Or no model pages at all — both make finding a product hard.
  • Certificates unexplained. Which models, which markets, and still valid?
  • OEM and brand mixed. Distributors and brand owners need different pages.

Questions overseas buyers ask Huizhou suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Can you send the datasheet and test report for this model?

Put datasheets beside each product page and say which test reports are available on request.

Which certifications do you hold for my market?

List certificates by market with scope and model coverage, and keep them current.

Can you build to our design?

Explain your OEM/ODM process, minimum orders and how you protect customer designs.

What is your monthly capacity and lead time?

Give realistic capacity ranges and lead times per product family.

How do you ship from Huizhou?

Name the ports you use — Huizhou, Shenzhen or Hong Kong — and your usual terms.

Are you the factory or a trading company?

Show your own lines, address, team and test equipment with your own photos.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Huizhou manufacturers

  1. Give each product family its own page with a specification table in HTML, not only a PDF or an image.
  2. Publish a certifications page by market and link it from every relevant product page.
  3. Separate OEM/ODM services from own-brand products so each buyer type finds its path.
  4. Add Product structured data where it fits the page and the facts are real.
  5. Show the factory, lines and test equipment with your own photos and short captions.
  6. Track RFQs by product family and country from the first week.
