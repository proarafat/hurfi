What Dongguan makes, according to official data

Dongguan’s own government documents describe a manufacturing base built on electronics, equipment and home products. That shapes where an export website should focus.

  • The municipal government describes new-generation electronic information as Dongguan’s largest pillar industry, with smart mobile terminals as its leading segment.[1]
  • Dongguan’s smart mobile terminal cluster was selected as a national advanced manufacturing cluster by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology in 2019. The city reports producing 196 million smartphones in 2022 — about one in six made in China.[1]
  • In January 2024 the city reported around 210,000 manufacturing enterprises and three national advanced manufacturing clusters: smart mobile terminals, smart equipment, and pan-home furnishing.[2]

What this means for a Dongguan manufacturer’s website

Electronics supply chain

For electronics and component suppliers, buyers want part data, compliance documents and traceability. A catalog that engineers can search beats a brochure.

OEM and ODM work

Factories that build for other brands need a site that proves capability without exposing client names they are not allowed to share.

Smart equipment

Equipment makers need specification pages, video of machines working, and a straight answer on service abroad.

Home and furniture products

Home-products manufacturers need range pages buyers can scan, with materials, finishes, MOQ and packaging information.

Where we would usually start

For a Dongguan manufacturer selling abroad, the first pages and search topics usually follow the city’s industrial mix:

  • Capability pages that describe processes — moulding, machining, assembly, finishing — with the equipment and tolerances you can publish.
  • An OEM or ODM page that explains confidentiality, tooling ownership and how a project moves from sample to mass production.
  • Separate pages for product categories you sell under your own brand and work you do for other brands.
  • Search content aimed at brand owners looking for a manufacturing partner by product type and process.
