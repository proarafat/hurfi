What Zhongshan makes, according to official data

The figures below come from Zhongshan’s 2025 statistical bulletin, published on the city government’s website.

  • Value added of industrial enterprises above designated size grew 5.4% in 2025. Advanced manufacturing grew 8.9% and made up 52.2% of the total.[1]
  • Equipment manufacturing grew 11.1% and accounted for 35.7%; electrical machinery and equipment grew 15.9% and computer, communications and other electronic equipment 10.2%.[1]
  • Household electrical appliances grew 4.6%, while furniture manufacturing fell 9.5%.[1]
  • Zhongshan produced about 1.1 billion lamps and lighting fixtures in 2025, up 2.4%; integrated-circuit output rose 28.5%.[1]
  • Goods exports were 251.56 billion yuan, up 1.0%, of which mechanical and electrical products were 200.64 billion yuan.[1]

Lighting and appliances are mature, competitive export categories where buyers see many similar catalogues. Growth is now stronger in electrical equipment and electronics. For a Zhongshan supplier, the website has to show what is different: specifications, certifications per market, design or OEM capability, and the newer product lines.

Zhongshan’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Lighting

Lighting buyers compare lumen output, efficacy, CRI, IP rating, driver and certification per market. Publish them in a table for each product family.

Household appliances

The fastest-growing lines need specification pages and test documents so engineers can shortlist you.

Furniture

Industrial zones and districts in Zhongshan

  • Zhongshan’s 2025 bulletin reports results by town and district: Xiaolan had the largest GDP (609.95 billion yuan), the Torch development area 587.49 billion yuan with above-scale industrial value added up 8.9%, and Guzhen 177.8 billion yuan.[1]
  • Guzhen calls itself the “Lighting Capital of China”. The 34th China (Guzhen) International Lighting Fair in March 2026 gathered more than 3,600 enterprises showing over 100,000 products, and nearly 70,000 registered buyers, including 4,500 international visitors from more than 130 countries and regions.[4]

Lighting is concentrated in Guzhen, appliances and hardware across several towns, and electronics and equipment in the Torch development area. A Zhongshan factory’s location often tells a buyer which cluster it belongs to — the website should say so.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Zhongshan’s ports handled 20.62 million tonnes of cargo (+6.2%) and 1.2557 million TEU (−2.9%) in 2025.[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • General-trade exports were 176.5 billion yuan (+4.3%) and processing-trade exports 66.96 billion yuan (−5.1%); high-tech product exports were 48.53 billion yuan (+2.8%).[1]
  • By market, exports to Belt and Road partners were 83.51 billion yuan (+6.5%), to the EU 43.91 billion yuan (+0.1%), to the United States 39.34 billion yuan (−18.0%), to ASEAN 32.73 billion yuan (+21.0%) and to Japan 9.23 billion yuan (+9.2%).[1]

Exports to the United States fell 18% in 2025 while exports to ASEAN rose 21%, a clear sign of buyers shifting. Typical products include lamps and lighting (about 1.1 billion units), fans, range hoods, refrigerators, air conditioners and other appliances, plus electrical equipment and furniture. Factories reaching new markets need certificates and specifications for each one.

Challenges Zhongshan manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Zhongshan.

  • Catalogue sameness. Thousands of lighting and appliance factories publish similar catalogues; specific specifications and certificates are what separate them.
  • Market shift. Falling US exports push factories toward ASEAN, the EU and other markets that need different certifications and voltages.
  • Fair-centred selling. Guzhen’s fairs bring buyers to town, but buyers who cannot travel rely on search and the website.
  • Image-heavy, slow sites. Lighting sites are often full of large images that load slowly abroad.

Questions overseas buyers ask Zhongshan suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Which certifications does this model have for our market?

Show certificates per model and market with the holder’s name, not a general logo row.

What are the real photometric or electrical values?

Publish measured values and the test standard, and offer test reports.

Can you put our brand on it?

Explain OEM options: logos, packaging, custom colours and MOQ for each.

What is the warranty and how are claims handled?

State warranty terms and the claim process clearly.

How do you pack for long-distance shipping?

Show packaging, carton specifications and drop-test practice if you have it.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Zhongshan manufacturers

  1. Organise products by family and application (for example commercial, outdoor or industrial lighting) rather than by internal series names.
  2. Give each family a specification table with measured values and test standards, plus downloadable test reports.
  3. Add an OEM page that lists customisation options, MOQs and sampling time.
  4. Write certification pages per market so buyers in Europe or North America see what applies to them.
  5. Write titles and descriptions that state the product and its key specification. Google’s title link guidance asks for descriptive, concise titles that avoid keyword stuffing.
  6. Ask for target market, quantity and certification needs in the RFQ form, so quotes are right first time.

Questions from Zhongshan manufacturers

Does Hurfi have an office in Zhongshan?

No. Hurfi does not have an office or staff in Zhongshan. We work with Zhongshan manufacturers remotely by WeChat, email and video, from Xi’an and Bogura.

Lighting is very competitive online. Is SEO still worth it?

Broad terms are hard to win, but specific searches — product type plus specification or application — are much more achievable, and they bring better-qualified buyers.

We have hundreds of models. Do they all need pages?

No. Give each family a page with a variant table. Hundreds of thin, near-identical pages are hard to maintain and rarely rank.

Should we show certificates on the website?

Yes, per model and market, with the certificate holder’s name. Only show certificates you actually hold.

Can you help us sell our own brand, not only OEM?

Yes. We help you present the brand separately from OEM supply, so retailers and brand buyers each see the right offer.

Do you work with appliance and furniture factories too?

Yes. The same principles apply: clear product families, specifications, compliance and packaging information.

Does a slow website hurt us?

It can. Buyers abroad leave slow pages, and product images are often the cause. We compress images and check speed from outside China.

Will you guarantee rankings?

No. Nobody can guarantee Google rankings. We commit to a clear plan and honest reporting.
