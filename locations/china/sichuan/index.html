Sichuan’s manufacturing economy in official figures

The Sichuan Provincial Bureau of Statistics’ 2025 bulletin, as published by Sichuan Daily’s Chuanguan News, gives the figures below.

  • Sichuan’s GDP was 6,766.53 billion yuan in 2025, up 5.5%; value added of industry above designated size grew 6.5%.[1]
  • Automobile manufacturing grew 16.7%, electrical machinery 13.2%, electronics 12.9% and chemicals 12.8%; high-tech manufacturing grew 12.3%.[1]
  • Output of smart TVs rose 72.7%, lithium-ion batteries 45.1%, cars 29.6% and LCD panels 21.6%.[1]
  • Goods trade was 1,031.81 billion yuan (−1.3%) and exports 608.65 billion yuan (−1.5%).[1]
  • Processing trade was 490.44 billion yuan (+3.1%), 47.5% of trade; general trade 34.5%.[1]
  • 33 of 41 industry groups grew; value added of foreign-invested firms rose 13.7%.[1]

Many Sichuan exports are processing trade for global brands. For the province’s own-brand and component makers, winning direct buyers means showing technical depth — and explaining logistics from western China clearly. Foreign-invested manufacturing grew fastest, and suppliers to those plants can use the same evidence to reach buyers abroad.

More Sichuan manufacturing cities, from their 2025 bulletins

Deyang

  • Deyang’s GDP was 338.71 billion yuan in 2025, up 5.4%.[2]
  • It made 76.106 million kW of power-generation equipment (+38.3%), including 40.2 million kW of power-station steam turbines (+26.4%) and 8.185 million kW of gas turbines (+20.0%).[2]
  • Exports fell 22.0% to 13.64 billion yuan.[2]
  • Revenue of above-designated-size industry was 455.08 billion yuan (+1.7%), but profits fell 9.8%.[2]

Deyang makes heavy power-generation equipment, and its output grew strongly in 2025. Its suppliers sell on standards, type tests and delivered projects; a website should show all three. Related: Electrical Equipment · Deyang in the China directory.

Website and search priorities for Sichuan exporters

  1. Give each product family a page with specifications and standards in text.
  2. Publish a logistics page for western China: rail, air and sea routes and terms.
  3. Build reference pages for delivered equipment you are allowed to show.
  4. Separate own-brand products from contract-manufacturing services.
  5. Show testing and quality systems with real photos.
  6. Track RFQs by product and country.

We make power equipment. What should the site show?

Ratings, standards, type tests, delivered projects and service, approved by your engineers.
