Wuxi’s manufacturers span equipment, semiconductors, IoT, automotive parts and new energy. Buyers abroad need to understand which of those you do, and how well. Hurfi helps Wuxi manufacturers say it clearly in English and get found in search.

What Wuxi makes, according to official data

Wuxi organises its industry policy around a named set of clusters. The city’s own figures show which of them are largest, which helps decide where an export website and search plan should start.

  • The Wuxi Industry and Information Technology Bureau reports that its “465” modern industrial cluster system included 5,582 above-designated-size enterprises in January–November 2025, with combined revenue of about RMB 1.7 trillion.[1]
  • The bureau expected eight clusters to exceed RMB 200 billion in revenue in 2025: high-end equipment, new materials, Internet of Things, software, integrated circuits, automobiles and parts, high-end textiles and apparel, and new energy.[1]

What this means for a Wuxi manufacturer’s website

High-end equipment

Equipment buyers want specifications, real machine video and a clear service answer. Those three things carry more weight than a long company history page.

Integrated circuits and IoT

Semiconductor and IoT suppliers need datasheets, compliance documents and integration details in HTML — readable by engineers and by search engines.

Automobiles and parts

Automotive-parts makers need pages that state the quality system and the standards each part meets, and only claim the approvals they hold.

New energy

For battery and storage suppliers, test conditions, certificates and shipping documents are the first questions a buyer asks.

Where we would usually start

For a Wuxi manufacturer selling abroad, the first pages and search topics usually follow the city’s industrial mix:

  • Equipment and component pages with technical data that integrators and OEM engineers can compare directly.
  • Pages for each export market that state which standards and certificates apply there.
  • Content that explains your role in the supply chain — tier, part type, customer industries — without naming clients you cannot share.
  • Search work around specification and application terms in the equipment, semiconductor, automotive-parts and new-energy sectors.
