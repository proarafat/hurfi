What Shenzhen makes, according to official data

Public statistics give a clear picture of what Shenzhen makes. We use them to decide which industry pages and search topics matter for a Shenzhen manufacturer — not as claims about any company.

  • Shenzhen’s 2025 statistical bulletin names computer, communications and other electronic equipment manufacturing first in its list of main industries, alongside automobile manufacturing, electrical machinery and equipment, and special-purpose and general-purpose equipment manufacturing.[1]
  • The same bulletin reports value added in computer, communications and electronic equipment manufacturing up 6.2% in 2025, and automobile manufacturing up 29.4%.[1]
  • The municipal Industry and Information Technology Bureau’s 2025 indicators show output growth of 43.1% for industrial robots, 40.1% for civilian drones and 45.1% for 3D printing equipment.[2]

What this means for a Shenzhen manufacturer’s website

Electronics and components

Most of Shenzhen’s industrial weight sits in electronics. For a component, module or PCBA maker, the website has to work like a parametric catalog — datasheets, compliance documents and an RFQ path that accepts a BOM.

Robotics and automation

With industrial-robot output growing fast, buyers abroad will compare several Shenzhen suppliers on the same day. Specification tables, protocol support and safety documentation decide who gets the inquiry.

Vehicles and electrical equipment

Automobile and electrical-equipment manufacturing are also large in the official data. EV component and power-equipment makers need market-specific pages that state which standards and certificates apply where.

Equipment and machinery

For general and special-purpose equipment makers, video of the machine working and a clear service answer abroad matter more than slogans.

Where we would usually start

For a Shenzhen manufacturer selling abroad, the first pages and search topics usually follow the city’s industrial mix:

  • Pages for each product family that a buyer would compare across Shenzhen suppliers, with specifications on the page rather than in a PDF.
  • A clear statement of which work happens in your own factory and which is done by partners in the Pearl River Delta supply chain.
  • Search work aimed at model numbers, interfaces and standards — the terms engineers use — before broad “Shenzhen manufacturer” phrases.
  • An English LinkedIn presence for the engineers and sales staff who answer overseas inquiries.
