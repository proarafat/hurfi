Jiangsu’s manufacturing economy in official figures

The Jiangsu Provincial Bureau of Statistics’ 2025 bulletin gives the province-wide picture below.

  • Jiangsu’s GDP was 14,235.15 billion yuan in 2025, up 5.3%, and value added of industry above designated size grew 6.5%.[1]
  • Equipment manufacturing grew 8.8% and made up 56% of above-designated-size industry; electronics grew 13.5%, general-purpose equipment 9.2% and rail, ship and aerospace equipment 15.8%.[1]
  • The province made 1.5748 million new-energy vehicles (+32.4%), 131,400 industrial robot sets (+31.5%) and 173.427 billion integrated circuits (+12.3%).[1]
  • Trade was 5.95 trillion yuan (+6%), 13.1% of China’s total; exports were 3.96 trillion yuan (+8.4%). Exports of integrated circuits grew 32.9%, ships 37% and electric vehicles 140.9%.[1]
  • Exports to ASEAN rose 28.9% to 749.92 billion yuan and to the EU 7.0% to 612.25 billion yuan, while exports to the US fell 25.8% to 438.43 billion yuan.[1]

Jiangsu’s exporters are shifting markets fast: sharply down in the US, sharply up in ASEAN and Africa. Buyers in those markets often discover suppliers through search, and they judge a technical supplier by the quality of its English product information.

More Jiangsu manufacturing cities, from their 2025 bulletins

These cities do not have a full guide yet. The figures come from each city’s 2025 statistical bulletin, listed in the sources below.

Xuzhou

  • Xuzhou’s GDP was 995.722 billion yuan in 2025 (+5.8%); value added of industry above designated size grew 6.8%.[2]
  • Exports were 89.463 billion yuan (−3.5%), of which mechanical and electrical products were 63.192 billion yuan (+4.4%); Belt and Road countries took 68.0% of exports, while exports to the US fell 41.6% and to the EU 20.9%.[2]
  • Xuzhou port handled 69.481 million tonnes of cargo (+15.7%) and 345,000 TEU of containers (+20.4%).[2]

Two-thirds of Xuzhou’s exports go to Belt and Road markets. Machinery and equipment suppliers there should build pages for those buyers — with parts, service and shipping answered up front. Related: Industrial Machinery & CNC · Xuzhou in the China directory.

Website and search priorities for Jiangsu exporters

  1. Publish specifications as HTML tables, with standards and test methods, for every product family.
  2. Add a quality and compliance page with certificates, test equipment and inspection steps.
  3. Write application pages for the industries you supply.
  4. Build market pages for ASEAN, the Middle East or Africa if those are where your orders are growing.
  5. Explain shipping and lead times from your nearest port.
  6. Track datasheet downloads and RFQs separately.
