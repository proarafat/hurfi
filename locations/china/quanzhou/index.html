Export websites and SEO for Quanzhou manufacturers and brands

Quanzhou is a private-manufacturing city: textiles and apparel, sports and craft goods, machinery, plastics and paper. Many of its factories make for other brands; some are building their own. Hurfi helps both kinds explain what they make and win buyers abroad.

Hurfi does not have an office or staff in Quanzhou. We work with Quanzhou manufacturers remotely — WeChat, email and video calls — from our offices in Xi’an (Shaanxi) and Bogura (Bangladesh).

What Quanzhou makes, according to official data

Quanzhou’s 2025 statistical bulletin and the city government’s interpretation of the year’s economy give the figures below.

  • Value added of industrial enterprises above designated size grew 7.0% in 2025, with manufacturing up 7.4% and light industry up 8.8%.[1]
  • Among the fastest-growing sectors were stationery, crafts, sports and entertainment goods (up 33.8%), general-purpose equipment (up 23.1%), textiles (up 19.3%), rubber and plastic products (up 19.3%) and paper (up 18.6%).[1]
  • The government’s interpretation says that eight of Quanzhou’s nine industries at the 100-billion-yuan level grew in 2025, with machinery and equipment up 11.0%, craft products 11.4% and paper and printing 13.4%.[2]

Light industry and consumer goods grew faster than heavy industry. For export, that often means OEM and ODM work for overseas brands, alongside factories building brands of their own. Buyers in both cases want to see design capability, materials, compliance and capacity — and they want to know whether you will work for them or compete with them.

Industrial zones and districts in Quanzhou

  • Jinjiang, a county-level city in Quanzhou, hosts the annual Jinjiang Footwear & Sports Industry International Exposition; the organiser calls Jinjiang the “Chinese Capital of Footwear”.[4]
  • The organiser says the exposition has a total exhibition area of more than 710,000 square metres and visitors from more than 70 countries and regions; the 27th edition ran from 19 to 22 April 2026.[4]

Footwear, sportswear and the materials and machinery behind them are concentrated in Jinjiang and neighbouring districts. Many of these firms make for global brands while building brands of their own.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Ports in Quanzhou handled 115.84 million tonnes of cargo (−0.9%), including 39.34 million tonnes of foreign-trade cargo, and 2.0646 million TEU (+1.1%) in 2025.[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Trade with Belt and Road partner countries was 165.58 billion yuan (−10.5%) and with other RCEP members 68.75 billion yuan (−19.0%) in 2025.[1]

Trade with Belt and Road and RCEP partners fell in 2025, even while light-industry output grew. That combination often pushes factories to look for new buyers and markets — which is when an export website and search visibility matter most. Typical products include footwear and sportswear, textiles, sports and craft goods, plastics, paper and packaging, and machinery.

Challenges Quanzhou manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Quanzhou.

  • OEM confidentiality. Factories cannot show the branded products they make, so they need to show development and production capability instead.
  • Own brand versus OEM. Mixing brand marketing and OEM supply on one site confuses both retailers and brand buyers.
  • Sustainability claims. Brand buyers ask for material and audit evidence; vague green claims backfire.
  • Shifting markets. Falling trade with some partners in 2025 means many exporters need to reach buyers in new regions.

Questions overseas buyers ask Quanzhou suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Can you develop our design, or only produce it?

Explain your design and development team, the sampling process and what you need from the brand.

What is your MOQ per style or colour?

Give ranges and explain what changes them.

Which materials and test reports can you provide?

List material options and the tests you can supply reports for.

Have you passed social-compliance audits?

Name the audits you have passed and their dates, where you are allowed to share them.

Do you sell your own brand in our market?

Be clear about it. Brand buyers worry about competing with their supplier.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Quanzhou manufacturers

  1. Decide what the website is for: OEM/ODM supply, your own brand, or both. If both, give each a clear section so brand buyers and retailers are not confused.
  2. Build pages for each product category with materials, processes, MOQ ranges and sampling time.
  3. Show development capability: design team, pattern or mould making, sampling room, with photos you own.
  4. Publish compliance and audit information carefully — the audit name, the date and the scope.
  5. Use LinkedIn to show real development and production work. A LinkedIn Page for the company gives buyers a second place to check you.
  6. Make the inquiry form ask for product type, quantity, target price range and timeline.

Questions

Questions from Quanzhou manufacturers

Does Hurfi have an office in Quanzhou?

No. We have no office or staff in Quanzhou. We work with Quanzhou manufacturers remotely by WeChat, email and video, from Xi’an and Bogura.

We make for other brands. Can we show our work online?

Yes, without naming clients: show categories, materials, processes, development steps and anonymised samples. Ask permission before showing any branded product.

Should our own brand and our OEM business share a website?

They can, if each has a clear section and message. Some companies use a separate brand site; the right choice depends on who your buyers are.

Is Google useful for apparel and footwear sourcing?

Yes, for searches by product, material and capability. Many sourcing managers search for specific constructions, materials or certifications.

Do you help with Instagram as well as LinkedIn?

Yes. Social media management covers LinkedIn for B2B buyers and, where it fits your brand, Instagram.

How do we present sustainability claims?

Only with evidence: certificates, material documents or test reports. Vague green claims can harm trust.

Should we publish MOQ and prices?

Publishing MOQ ranges helps qualify buyers. Prices usually depend on materials and quantity, so explain the drivers instead.

Can you promise orders?

No. We work to bring qualified inquiries and report them from your own accounts. Orders depend on your products, prices and follow-up.
