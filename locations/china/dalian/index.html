Export websites and Google SEO for Dalian manufacturers

Dalian is a port city at the tip of the Liaodong peninsula with shipyards, petrochemicals, equipment, vehicles and a growing chip industry. Many of its suppliers sell to Japan, Korea and beyond. Hurfi helps Dalian manufacturers present heavy, technical products clearly to international buyers.

Hurfi does not have an office or staff in Dalian. We work with Dalian manufacturers remotely — WeChat, email and video calls — from our offices in Xi’an (Shaanxi) and Bogura (Bangladesh).

What Dalian makes, according to official data

Dalian’s 2025 statistical bulletin and the State Council approval of the Jinpu New Area give the figures below.

  • Value added of industry above designated size grew 11.7% in 2025, with high-tech manufacturing up 13.9%, equipment manufacturing 15.4% and the petrochemical industry 8.9%.[1]
  • Dalian completed 4.395 million deadweight tonnes of civilian steel ships, up 74.6%, and 300,000 vehicles, up 29.5%; integrated-circuit wafer output rose 19.1%.[1]
  • Goods trade was 449.26 billion yuan (−0.3%), while exports grew 10.6% to 243.25 billion yuan.[1]
  • Port cargo throughput was 300 million tonnes and container throughput 5.482 million TEU, up 1.5%.[1]
  • Singapore, the UAE, the Cayman Islands, Hong Kong (China) and Japan were the top five sources of foreign investment actually used.[1]

Dalian’s fastest growth is in ships and equipment — products sold through long, technical procurement processes. Buyers check certifications, classification-society approvals, references and engineering capacity long before they ask for a price. A Dalian supplier’s website is often the first place that checking happens.

Dalian’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Shipbuilding and marine equipment

Marine buyers check class approvals, materials, welding procedures and delivered projects. Publish what you are allowed to.

Petrochemicals and materials

Chemical buyers ask for grades, specifications, SDS and packaging.

Equipment and machinery

Automotive

Semiconductors

Chip-related suppliers need process, quality and qualification information in searchable text.

Industrial zones and districts in Dalian

  • Dalian Jinpu New Area, approved by the State Council in 2014, covers about 2,299 square kilometres, including all of Jinzhou District and part of Pulandian.[2]
  • The approval positions the new area as a strategic base for open cooperation with Northeast Asia and supports Dalian as a Northeast Asian shipping and logistics centre.[2]

Many of Dalian’s export manufacturers sit in Jinpu and around the port. Buyers in Japan, Korea and further afield want to see approvals, references and shipping capability, so put those up front.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Port cargo throughput was 300 million tonnes in 2025 (−6.2%) and containers 5.482 million TEU (+1.5%); air cargo was 60,000 tonnes (+12.0%).[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • By trade type, processing trade grew 4.6% to 113.68 billion yuan while general trade fell 6.3%; foreign-invested firms’ trade grew 4.8%.[1]

Dalian’s ties to Japan and Korea are strong — Japan is among its top sources of foreign investment. Japanese- or Korean-language pages can help where you already sell, if they are well translated and tagged.

Challenges Dalian manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Dalian.

  • Slow, heavy sites. Big images and old systems make pages slow for buyers abroad.
  • Approvals not listed. Marine and chemical buyers check class and registrations first.
  • No reference projects. Equipment buyers want proof from delivered work.
  • Northeast Asian markets underserved. English-only sites miss buyers who search in Japanese or Korean.

Questions overseas buyers ask Dalian suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Which classification societies approve your products?

List approvals per product with certificate numbers where possible.

What projects have you delivered?

Describe permitted projects with facts: type, size, year, scope.

What is your engineering capacity?

Explain design, testing and production capacity in numbers you can stand behind.

Do you serve Japanese or Korean buyers?

If so, say which documents and languages you support.

How do you ship oversized items?

Describe port access, packing and shipping methods.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Dalian manufacturers

  1. Publish a certification and approvals page, and link each approval to the products it covers.
  2. Build project pages for permitted references with specifications and photos.
  3. Add an engineering capability page — design tools, test facilities, welding and inspection processes.
  4. Consider Japanese or Korean language pages if those buyers matter, with proper language annotations.
  5. Keep pages fast and readable: Web Vitals set good thresholds of 2.5 s for LCP, 200 ms for INP and 0.1 for CLS.
  6. Track RFQs by product type and country to see which markets respond.
