Export websites and Google SEO for Shenyang manufacturers

Shenyang is the capital of Liaoning and one of China’s oldest equipment-making cities: machine tools and general equipment, vehicles, electrical machinery, aerospace and transport equipment, and robots. Its exports grew in 2025 while overall industry fell. Hurfi helps Shenyang manufacturers find buyers abroad with websites that explain their engineering clearly.

Hurfi does not have an office or staff in Shenyang. We work with Shenyang manufacturers remotely — WeChat, email and video calls — from our offices in Xi’an (Shaanxi) and Bogura (Bangladesh).

What Shenyang makes, according to official data

Shenyang’s 2025 statistical bulletin, published on the city government portal, gives the figures below.

  • Shenyang’s GDP was 910.03 billion yuan in 2025, up 2.0%; value added of industry above designated size fell 6.0%.[1]
  • Equipment manufacturing made up 68.0% of above-designated-size industrial value added, though it fell 7.4% in the year.[1]
  • Electrical machinery and equipment grew 15.4% and rail, ship, aerospace and other transport equipment 3.1%, while automobile manufacturing fell 10.3% and general-purpose equipment 8.1%.[1]
  • The city produced 5,170 industrial robot sets (+4.2%), and lithium-ion battery output rose 57.5%.[1]
  • Goods trade was 135.37 billion yuan (−7.6%), but exports rose 8.8% to 63.33 billion yuan, with general-trade exports up 25.5%.[1]

With two-thirds of industry in equipment and domestic demand weaker, exports are where Shenyang’s growth came from in 2025. Equipment buyers abroad want to understand the machine, the company’s engineering record and the after-sales arrangements before they talk price. Those answers belong on the website.

Shenyang’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Machine tools and general equipment

Machine buyers compare working range, accuracy, controls and service. One page per machine family with real specifications.

Electrical machinery

Electrical-equipment buyers check ratings, standards and test reports per product series.

Aerospace and transport equipment

Transport-equipment buyers need approvals, materials traceability and references, written carefully.

Robots and automation

Robot buyers look for payload, reach, repeatability and integration support.

Vehicles and parts

Parts suppliers should show platforms served, quality systems and validation.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Shenyang’s airport had 222 routes at the end of 2025, 21 of them international, and handled 24.938 million passengers.[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • General-trade exports rose 25.5% to 51.12 billion yuan, while processing-trade exports fell 39.5% to 8.88 billion yuan.[1]

Shenyang’s export growth came from general trade — firms selling their own products directly. That is exactly the kind of exporter that needs its own strong website.

Challenges Shenyang manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Shenyang.

  • Old sites for strong companies. Long-established equipment makers often have outdated sites that undersell them.
  • No after-sales information. Buyers abroad worry most about service.
  • Machines without video. Photos alone do not prove a machine works.
  • Technical text not checked. Mistranslated specifications cost credibility.

Questions overseas buyers ask Shenyang suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

What accuracy and working range does this machine have?

Publish them per model in a table, with the test standard used.

Who installs and services the machine in our country?

Explain remote support, training, spare parts and any service partners honestly.

Can we see the machine working?

Offer videos of real machines and an option for a video call from the shop floor.

How long does a custom machine take?

Give typical ranges for standard and custom builds.

How is the machine packed and shipped?

Describe packing, crating and the port you ship from.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Shenyang manufacturers

  1. Give each machine family a page with working range, accuracy, power, controls and options in HTML tables.
  2. Publish an after-sales page: installation, training, spare-parts stock and remote support, with honest response times.
  3. Add short videos of machines in operation, with captions that say what the machine is doing.
  4. Write application pages by industry served, explaining which machine and options fit each job.
  5. Follow Google’s guidance on video best practices so machine videos can be found in search.
  6. Track RFQs by machine family and country, and expand the pages that work.
