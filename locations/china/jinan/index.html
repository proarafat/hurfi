What Jinan makes, according to official data

Jinan’s 2025 statistical bulletin gives the figures below.

  • Value added of industry above designated size grew 6.9% in 2025: computers, communications and electronic equipment grew 37.8%, automobile manufacturing 19.8% and general-purpose equipment 7.3%; equipment manufacturing grew 16.8%.[1]
  • Electronics had the largest industrial revenue, 227.59 billion yuan (+39.5%), followed by automobile manufacturing at 177.82 billion yuan (+16.2%).[1]
  • Jinan produced 298,000 trucks (+19.6%), 968,000 servers (+3.5%) and 23,000 metal-cutting machine tools (+8.9%); generator-set output rose 24.1%.[1]
  • Goods trade was 289.80 billion yuan (+24.7%) and exports 195.71 billion yuan (+24.2%); mechanical and electrical exports rose 29% and steel products 46.4%.[1]
  • Trade with ASEAN and Latin America grew 30.6% and 30.3%, and 1,252 China–Europe Railway Express trains ran from Jinan, up 21.7%.[1]

Jinan’s exports are growing fastest to ASEAN and Latin America, in trucks, machinery, electronics and steel. These are buyers who compare specifications and service before price. A Jinan supplier whose site answers those questions in clear English — and in Spanish where it matters — has an advantage.

Jinan’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Electronics and servers

Server and electronics buyers look for configurations, certifications and support terms.

Trucks, engines and parts

Truck and parts buyers check models, engine data, certifications and parts supply.

Machine tools and equipment

Machine-tool buyers compare travel, spindle, accuracy, controls and service.

Power equipment

Generator and transformer buyers check ratings, standards and test reports.

Industrial zones and districts in Jinan

  • The bulletin reports progress on the Jinan start-up area for new and old growth drivers, including a China Telecom (Jinan) computing centre.[1]

Jinan’s growth in servers, electronics and trucks sits alongside new computing infrastructure. Buyers of these products are technical; clear specifications and service information matter more than slogans.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Jinan ran 1,252 China–Europe Railway Express trains in 2025 (+21.7%), including Shandong’s first scheduled service to Budapest; the airport handled 174,000 tonnes of cargo and mail (+13.1%).[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Mechanical and electrical exports were 129.43 billion yuan (+29%), steel products 14.26 billion yuan (+46.4%) and high-tech products 31.19 billion yuan (+11.2%).[1]

Trade with ASEAN and Latin America grew by more than 30% in 2025, and rail links to Europe expanded. Spanish-language pages or ASEAN-focused content can make sense for suppliers already selling there.

Challenges Jinan manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Jinan.

  • Heavy equipment shown without service detail. Truck and machinery buyers ask about parts and service first.
  • Server and electronics specs hard to find. Buyers want parameters in text tables.
  • No market-specific pages. Growth markets like Latin America need their own content.
  • Unmeasured websites. Without Search Console, a supplier cannot see what works.

Questions overseas buyers ask Jinan suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Do you have service partners in Latin America or Southeast Asia?

Say where you have support and how parts reach the buyer.

Which controls and components do you use?

Name main component brands where you can.

What tests does each machine pass before shipping?

Describe factory acceptance tests and what reports buyers receive.

Can you send a quotation with Incoterms?

State which Incoterms you quote and typical lead times.

Do you have Spanish documentation?

Say which documents exist in which languages.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Jinan manufacturers

  1. Create machine and model pages with full specification tables and component lists.
  2. Add a service and parts page by region, written honestly.
  3. Add Spanish pages for Latin American buyers if they matter to you, set up with proper language tags.
  4. Publish factory acceptance test procedures and sample reports.
  5. Register the site in Search Console and watch which countries and queries bring impressions.
  6. Track RFQs by model and country.
