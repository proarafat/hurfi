Export websites and Google SEO for Taizhou, Zhejiang manufacturers

Taizhou in Zhejiang is a city of private manufacturers: pumps and general equipment, motorcycles and cars, moulds, plastics, shoes, eyewear and medicines. Private firms made nearly nine-tenths of its exports in 2025. Hurfi helps these makers sell beyond trading companies with websites buyers trust.

Hurfi does not have an office or staff in Taizhou. We work with Taizhou manufacturers remotely — WeChat, email and video calls — from our offices in Xi’an (Shaanxi) and Bogura (Bangladesh).

What Taizhou makes, according to official data

The figures below come from Taizhou’s 2025 statistical bulletin.

  • Value added of Taizhou’s industry above designated size was 184.965 billion yuan in 2025, up 7.4%.[1]
  • The city made 226,900 cars (+72.6%), 1.4979 million motorcycles and 64.0521 million pumps.[1]
  • It also made 256.034 million pairs of shoes, 202.2984 million pairs of eyeglasses and 105,500 sets of moulds.[1]
  • Exports were 255.348 billion yuan (+7.0%); private firms made 89.3% of them.[1]
  • Exports to the EU rose 6.3% to 52.331 billion yuan, while exports to the United States fell 14.4% to 30.398 billion yuan.[1]
  • General-purpose equipment made 31.116 billion yuan of value added (+6.7%) and automobiles 24.640 billion yuan (+31.7%); equipment manufacturing grew 13.6%.[1]

Most Taizhou exporters are private, mid-sized and specialised: one product family, made very well. Buyers searching for that family compare dozens of suppliers. The ones with clear model tables, certifications per market and real factory proof are the ones who get the RFQ. The number of exporting firms grew again in 2025, so the comparison buyers make is getting harder, not easier.

Taizhou’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Pumps and fluid equipment

Pump buyers compare flow, head, power, materials and certifications in a model table. Give curves and datasheets per series.

Motorcycles, cars and parts

Vehicle and parts buyers check homologation, standards and quality systems per market.

Moulds and plastics

Mould buyers want capability, materials, tolerances, tool life and examples of tools delivered.

Shoes and eyewear

Brand buyers check compliance, capacity per style and audits you hold.

Pharmaceuticals and chemicals

Pharma buyers check GMP status, registrations and documentation exactly as held.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Taizhou’s port handled 100.186 million tonnes of cargo in 2025 (+17.6%) and 856,800 TEU of containers.[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Private firms exported 227.979 billion yuan, 89.3% of Taizhou’s exports.[1]
  • Taizhou exported to 237 countries and regions in 2025.[1]
  • General trade made up 94.1% of exports, and 9,418 Taizhou firms had export records — 946 more than a year earlier.[1]

Taizhou’s EU exports grew while US exports fell sharply in 2025. A website with EU certifications in order and pages for other growing markets helps spread the risk.

Challenges Taizhou manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Taizhou.

  • Looking like a trader. Buyers cannot tell you are the factory.
  • Model data in PDFs only. Buyers want a searchable table.
  • Certifications missing by market. Especially for vehicles, pumps and medicines.
  • No applications shown. Buyers want to see where products work.

Questions overseas buyers ask Taizhou suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Can you send the performance curve and datasheet?

Put curves and datasheets on each series page.

Which certifications cover our market?

List them by market with model coverage.

Can you make our design or brand?

Explain OEM, tooling, MOQ and design protection.

Are you a factory or a trading company?

Show your factory, lines and team, with your own photos and address.

What are your lead times?

Give realistic ranges per product family.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Taizhou manufacturers

  1. Make it obvious on the first screen that you are the manufacturer, with real factory photos.
  2. Give each series a page with an HTML model table and downloadable datasheets.
  3. Publish a certifications page by market.
  4. Explain OEM and tooling services on their own page.
  5. Show where your products are used, with real applications and photos.
  6. Track RFQs by product series and country.
