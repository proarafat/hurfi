Export websites and Google SEO for Jiaxing manufacturers

Jiaxing lies between Shanghai, Hangzhou and Suzhou and makes electronics, chemicals and new materials, electrical and general equipment, and textiles and fashion goods. Its port’s container traffic grew faster than any other Zhejiang coastal port in 2025. Hurfi helps Jiaxing makers turn that position into overseas buyers.

Hurfi does not have an office or staff in Jiaxing. We work with Jiaxing manufacturers remotely — WeChat, email and video calls — from our offices in Xi’an (Shaanxi) and Bogura (Bangladesh).

What Jiaxing makes, according to official data

The figures below come from Jiaxing’s 2025 statistical bulletin.

  • Jiaxing’s GDP was 785.106 billion yuan in 2025, up 5.2%; value added of industry above designated size was 293.977 billion yuan (+5.4%).[1]
  • Computer and communication equipment grew 10.3%, chemicals 8.8%, electrical machinery 7.2% and general-purpose equipment 7.0%.[1]
  • High-tech manufacturing value added was 55.046 billion yuan (+13.6%) and equipment manufacturing 142.296 billion yuan (+7.9%); fashion manufacturing fell 1.3%.[1]
  • Private firms made 65.5% of above-designated-size industrial value added.[1]
  • Exports were 361.959 billion yuan (+0.7%); mechanical and electrical products made up 48.2% and high-tech products 14.5%.[1]

Jiaxing’s exports held steady in 2025 while its high-tech output grew. Many of its suppliers sell into the Shanghai and Suzhou supply chains as well as abroad, so their buyers are often engineers and purchasing teams comparing specifications. Clear, specific websites win those comparisons. Foreign investment in its manufacturing also grew strongly in 2025, so many local suppliers now serve foreign-owned plants nearby as well as buyers abroad.

Jiaxing’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Electronics and communication equipment

Electronics buyers want part numbers, parameters, standards and quality systems in searchable text.

Chemicals and new materials

Materials buyers look for grades, specifications, SDS and packaging per product.

Electrical and general equipment

Equipment buyers compare ratings, standards, type tests and service options.

Textiles and fashion

Textile and fashion buyers check composition, compliance, capacity and sampling.

General-purpose machinery

Machine buyers compare output, power, automation and service, one machine family at a time.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Jiaxing port handled 143.5581 million tonnes of cargo in 2025 (+1.8%), including 19.3586 million tonnes of foreign-trade cargo (+12.8%).[1]
  • Its container throughput was 4.255 million TEU, up 14.9%, the fastest growth among Zhejiang’s coastal ports.[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Trade with Belt and Road countries was 264.52 billion yuan (+11.3%), and exports to them 184.07 billion yuan (+9.7%).[1]
  • The export delivery value of above-designated-size industry fell 3.7% to 276.077 billion yuan.[1]
  • Actual use of foreign investment was 2.665 billion US dollars, second in Zhejiang; manufacturing took 1.22 billion (+36.4%), 45.8% of the total.[1]

Jiaxing’s exports to Belt and Road countries grew while total exports were flat. Buyers in those markets need clear English, certifications that match their rules and realistic shipping information.

Challenges Jiaxing manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Jiaxing.

  • Generic first screen. Buyers cannot see what you make.
  • Specifications in PDFs only. They should also be in the page text.
  • Port and terms not explained. Buyers ask anyway — answer first.
  • Mixed audiences. Domestic and export buyers need different pages.

Questions overseas buyers ask Jiaxing suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Can you send the specification and test report?

Put specifications beside each product page and say which reports are available.

Which certifications do you hold for my market?

List them by market with scope and products covered.

Can you supply both our China and overseas plants?

Explain how you serve customers in China and abroad, with terms for each.

What is your capacity?

Give realistic ranges per product family.

Which port do you ship from?

Name Jiaxing, Shanghai or Ningbo as you use them, with typical terms.

Can your engineers support a new project?

Describe your engineering team, sampling and how you handle new specifications.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Jiaxing manufacturers

  1. Put what you make, for which industry and to which standards on the first screen.
  2. Give each product family a page with an HTML specification table.
  3. Publish a certifications page by market and link it from product pages.
  4. Write a logistics page: ports used, packing and terms.
  5. Use real photos of production and testing, with short captions.
  6. Track RFQs by product family and country.
