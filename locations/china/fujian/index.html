Export websites and Google SEO for Fujian manufacturers

Fujian makes electronics, machinery and electrical equipment in Xiamen, textiles, shoes, apparel and sports goods in Quanzhou, textiles, steel and vehicles in Fuzhou, and lithium batteries in Ningde. Its exports fell in 2025, but sales to the EU grew. This guide summarises the province’s official figures and links to our city guides.

Fujian’s manufacturing economy in official figures

The Fujian Provincial Bureau of Statistics’ 2025 bulletin gives the province-wide picture below.

  • Fujian’s GDP was 6,019.945 billion yuan in 2025, up 5.0%; value added of industry above designated size grew 6.5%.[1]
  • Computer, communication and other electronic equipment grew 16.9%, electrical machinery 14.0% and ferrous metals 13.1%; high-tech manufacturing grew 14.6% and equipment manufacturing 13.9%.[1]
  • Goods trade was 1,882.699 billion yuan (−5.4%) and exports 1,155.362 billion yuan (−6.7%); mechanical and electrical exports rose 2.2% to 545.593 billion yuan.[1]
  • Exports to the EU rose 9.7% to 222.047 billion yuan, while exports to ASEAN fell 18.0% and to the United States 22.1%.[1]
  • Fujian’s ports handled 18.4364 million TEU of containers, up 1.8%.[1]
  • The province made 275,900 cars (+25.2%) and 7.8863 million tonnes of chemical fibre; general-trade exports fell 6.9% while processing-trade exports rose 7.6%.[1]

Fujian’s exporters are mostly private and highly specialised, and 2025 pushed many of them towards new buyers and markets. Europe was the bright spot. Selling there means certifications in order, real product data and a website that answers questions before they are asked. Processing trade and mechanical and electrical exports both grew, a sign of where demand was holding up.

More Fujian manufacturing cities, from their 2025 bulletins

These cities do not have a full guide yet. The figures come from each city’s 2025 statistical bulletin, listed in the sources below.

Ningde

  • Ningde’s value added of industry above designated size grew 13.2% in 2025; lithium-battery new energy, new-energy vehicles, stainless steel and copper made 83.5% of it.[2]
  • Lithium-battery new energy grew 17.7%, stainless steel 16.5% and copper materials 21.3%, while new-energy vehicles fell 3.7%.[2]
  • Exports rose 12.6% to 113.30 billion yuan; lithium-ion battery exports grew 17.1%, exports to the EU 66.4%, and exports to the US fell 13.5%.[2]
  • Private firms made 77.6% of Ningde’s trade, and mechanical and electrical exports grew 16.6%.[2]

Ningde is a battery and materials city. Suppliers into its battery chain should publish specifications, test reports and transport certifications clearly; Europe’s fast-growing demand also brings strict documentation rules. Related: Battery & Energy Storage · Ningde in the China directory.

Website and search priorities for Fujian exporters

  1. Put what you make, for whom and to which standards on the first screen.
  2. Give each product family a page with specifications in text and certificates by market.
  3. For Europe, prepare CE and other required documents before you build market pages.
  4. Separate own-brand and OEM pages; many Fujian makers do both.
  5. Explain shipping from Xiamen, Fuzhou or your nearest port.
  6. Track RFQs by product line and country to see which new markets respond.
