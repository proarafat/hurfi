What Foshan makes, according to official data

The figures below come from Foshan’s 2025 statistical bulletin, as published by Southern Plus (Nanfang Daily).

  • Foshan’s GDP was 1,315.735 billion yuan in 2025, up 0.2%, and value added of industry above designated size fell 4.3%.[1]
  • Advanced manufacturing made up 55.7% of above-designated-size industrial value added, 3.2 points more than a year earlier; advanced equipment manufacturing grew 0.7% and biomedicine and high-performance medical devices 6.4%.[1]
  • Among traditional industries, household electrical appliances grew 6.8%, while furniture fell 6.7%, building materials 14.3% and metal products 13.0%.[1]
  • Exports were 373.11 billion yuan (−3.7%). Mechanical and electrical products reached 242.0 billion yuan (+5.5%), home appliances 65.36 billion yuan (−5.4%) and furniture 18.93 billion yuan (+1.1%).[1]
  • Exports to the United States fell 21.2% to 51.27 billion yuan; exports to the EU were 53.37 billion yuan (−0.7%).[1]

The picture is a mature industrial city under pressure in its traditional lines and moving towards equipment, appliances and medical products. With US orders down by a fifth, Foshan exporters need buyers in other markets, and those buyers usually find a supplier through search, not through a long-standing agent.

Foshan’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Home appliances

Appliance buyers check models, voltages, plug types and certification marks per market, plus OEM options. Put them on each model page.

Furniture and interiors

Furniture buyers look for materials, finishes, test standards, packing and container loading. Show real room sets and real cartons.

Machinery and equipment

Equipment buyers compare capacity, power, controls and spare parts. Write the specifications as text, not inside catalogue images.

Building materials and ceramics

Tile, sanitary and building-material buyers need sizes, ratings, standards and project references they can show their own clients.

Aluminium and metal products

Profile and metal-product buyers search by alloy, temper, finish and section. Searchable section libraries bring better RFQs.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Foshan’s main ports handled 105.966 million tonnes of cargo in 2025 (−2.8%), including 3.5929 million TEU of containers (−4.4%).[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • General trade was 392.85 billion yuan (−2.5%) and processing trade 80.87 billion yuan (−10.9%); high-tech product exports were 21.59 billion yuan (+9.4%).[1]
  • Exports to Hong Kong (China) were 24.52 billion yuan (−12.2%).[1]

Exports to the US fell sharply and to the EU slightly. That makes other regions — and new buyers in existing ones — more important. Market pages, certifications and shipping information for those regions help them find and trust you.

Challenges Foshan manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Foshan.

  • Catalogues as PDFs. Hundreds of products, none of them readable by Google.
  • US-dependent pipelines. Sites built around a few large US buyers do not attract new ones elsewhere.
  • Showroom photos without captions. Buyers want to know what the product is, its size and where it was used.
  • Packing and loading not explained. Furniture and appliance buyers ask about cartons and container loads early.

Questions overseas buyers ask Foshan suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Can you ship to our market with the right plugs and marks?

List voltages, plug types and certification marks per model and market.

How do you pack for long sea freight?

Show packing specifications, carton tests and container loading quantities.

Can we mix models in one container?

Say how mixed loads work, with MOQs per model or colour.

Do you have project references?

Give references you are allowed to share, with photos and the products used.

What happens if goods arrive damaged?

Explain your claims process and spare-parts policy in plain language.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Foshan manufacturers

  1. Rebuild the product catalogue as web pages: one page per series, with dimensions, materials and certifications in text.
  2. Add market pages for the regions you want to grow in, written for those buyers, not machine-translated copies of the Chinese site.
  3. Publish a packing and logistics page with carton specifications, container quantities and shipping ports.
  4. Show project and showroom photos with captions that say what the product is and where it was used, with permission.
  5. Follow Google’s guidance on product structured data for product pages you want to stand out in search.
  6. Measure which series and markets bring RFQs and expand those first; keep the rest simple.

Questions from Foshan manufacturers

Does Hurfi have an office in Foshan?

No. Hurfi does not have an office or staff in Foshan. We work with Foshan manufacturers remotely by WeChat, email and video, from our offices in Xi’an and Bogura.

Our US orders dropped. Can a website help us find other markets?

A website cannot replace lost demand on its own, but market pages, search work and campaigns aimed at chosen regions can help new buyers find you. We plan them with you market by market.

We sell through distributors. Should we still have a strong website?

Yes. Distributors and their customers check the factory online. A clear site supports your partners and shows new ones what you offer.

Our catalogue is a 200-page PDF. Is that enough?

Keep the PDF for download, but buyers and Google need the products as web pages with text specifications they can search.

Can you show our showroom and factory on the site?

Yes. Real photos and short videos of showrooms, lines and packing are among the strongest trust signals for furniture and appliance buyers.

Which certifications should we show?

The ones you actually hold, on the product pages they cover, with the market each applies to.

Do you write in Spanish or Arabic too?

We can add language versions for markets you sell to, set up with the right language tags. We start with English.

Can you guarantee more orders?

No. We plan for more qualified inquiries and report honestly from your own accounts.
