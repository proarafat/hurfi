What Yantai makes, according to official data

Yantai’s 2025 statistical bulletin gives the figures below.

  • Yantai’s GDP was 1,135.098 billion yuan in 2025, up 6.1%, and value added of industry above designated size grew 13.5%, with manufacturing up 16.1%.[1]
  • Among the ten largest industries, chemical raw materials and products grew 46.2%, non-ferrous metal smelting and rolling 15.7%, general-purpose equipment 8.8% and agricultural and food processing 6.7%.[1]
  • Equipment manufacturing made up 31.2% of above-designated-size industrial value added, and high-tech industry output 62.45% of above-designated-size industrial output.[1]
  • Coastal ports handled 544.127 million tonnes of cargo (+8.4%), including 192.11 million tonnes of foreign-trade cargo, and 5.5596 million TEU of containers (+9.2%).[1]
  • Goods trade was 537.932 billion yuan (+13.7%), with exports of 296.861 billion yuan (+7.1%).[1]

Yantai’s growth is led by chemicals, metals and equipment — products sold on specifications, grades and standards. Its buyers are technical purchasers who compare suppliers across countries. The suppliers that answer their questions in clear English, with data they can check, get shortlisted.

Yantai’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Chemicals and materials

Chemical buyers need grades, specifications, SDS and packaging per product, plus the registrations you hold.

Non-ferrous metals

Metal buyers search by alloy, form, size and standard, and ask for mill test certificates. Publish them per product.

General equipment

Equipment buyers compare capacity, power and service abroad. Make the specifications searchable.

Vehicles and parts

Parts buyers check part numbers, materials and quality systems. Searchable part data helps.

Food processing

Food buyers check certifications, shelf life, packaging and export registrations before price.

Industrial zones and districts in Yantai

  • The bulletin says the Oriental Aerospace Port has supported 22 sea-launch missions carrying 137 satellites.[1]

Yantai combines heavy chemicals and metals with newer aerospace and equipment work. Buyers of all of them expect technical detail they can verify.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Yantai’s ports had 240 production berths, 123 of them for ships of 10,000 tonnes or more, at the end of 2025.[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Trade with Belt and Road countries was 299.05 billion yuan and with other RCEP members 181.73 billion yuan; processing-trade exports rose 42.6%.[1]

Yantai trades heavily with RCEP partners including Korea and Japan, and with Belt and Road markets. Plan language versions only for the markets you really serve.

Challenges Yantai manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Yantai.

  • Grades without data. Chemical and metal buyers leave when they cannot see the specification.
  • SDS and certificates on request only. Buyers want to check before they write.
  • Port strength not mentioned. Shipping capability is a selling point; say it.
  • Korean and Japanese buyers missed. English-only sites can lose them.

Questions overseas buyers ask Yantai suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Can you send the specification and SDS for this grade?

Offer both as downloads beside the product text.

Which standards does this alloy or product meet?

Name the standards per product and the test certificates you provide.

What packaging and minimum quantities do you offer?

State pack sizes, MOQs and pallet or container loads.

Which port do you ship from?

Name the port, typical transit times and the Incoterms you quote.

Do you have export registrations for food?

List the registrations and certificates you actually hold, per market.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Yantai manufacturers

  1. Give every grade or product a page with its specification table, standards and downloadable SDS or test certificate sample.
  2. Write application pages that explain which grade suits which use, in the language buyers search with.
  3. Publish a packaging and shipping page with port, pack sizes and container quantities.
  4. Add a quality and laboratory page showing test equipment and the certificates you issue with each shipment.
  5. Use Google’s guidance on product structured data where it fits your product pages.
  6. Track sample requests and RFQs separately so you can see which grades attract serious buyers.
