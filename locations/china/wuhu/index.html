Export websites and Google SEO for Wuhu manufacturers

Wuhu, on the Yangtze in Anhui, is a car city and a maker of robots, compressors, air conditioners, rail vehicles, ships and moulds. Its exports grew more than a third in 2025. Hurfi helps Wuhu suppliers reach the buyers and engineers who now look at Anhui.

Hurfi does not have an office or staff in Wuhu. We work with Wuhu manufacturers remotely — WeChat, email and video calls — from our offices in Xi’an (Shaanxi) and Bogura (Bangladesh).

What Wuhu makes, according to official data

The figures below come from Wuhu’s 2025 statistical bulletin.

  • Wuhu’s GDP was 540.269 billion yuan in 2025, up 6.3%; equipment manufacturing made up 66.3% of above-designated-size industrial value added and grew 15.9%.[1]
  • The city made 1.801 million cars (+10.2%), including 1.364 million SUVs, and 411,000 new-energy vehicles (+37.6%).[1]
  • It made 19,241 industrial robot sets, 75.263 million compressors and 16.996 million air conditioners.[1]
  • It also built 138 urban rail vehicles and ships of 796,000 deadweight tonnes.[1]
  • Exports were 164.61 billion yuan, up 36.7%.[1]
  • Rail, ship, aerospace and other transport equipment grew 33.6%, electronics 26.6%, metal products 23.8% and automobiles 19.7%; lithium-ion battery output rose 74.8%.[1]

Wuhu’s exporters range from vehicle and parts makers to robot, compressor and appliance suppliers. Many sell into global car and appliance supply chains, where buyers check quality systems, testing and capacity long before they ask for a price. Battery and electronics output is growing even faster than cars, adding new supplier categories.

Wuhu’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Cars, EVs and parts

Vehicle and parts buyers check quality systems, testing and certifications per market. Name vehicle types, not customers you cannot name.

Industrial robots and automation

Robot buyers compare payload, reach, repeatability, controllers and integration support.

Compressors and air conditioning

HVAC buyers compare capacities, refrigerants, efficiency and certifications in model tables.

Rail and marine equipment

Transport-equipment buyers check standards, approvals and project references.

Moulds and precision parts

Mould and parts buyers want tolerances, materials, tool life and examples.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Wuhu’s port handled 165 million tonnes of cargo in 2025 (+6.4%) and 1.8126 million TEU of containers (+11.3%).[1]
  • Rail freight dispatched rose 49.5% to 4.0582 million tonnes.[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Exports rose 36.7% to 164.61 billion yuan in 2025.[1]

Wuhu’s exports grew fast in 2025, much of it with its vehicle industry. Suppliers following that growth abroad need certifications and documentation that match each market’s rules.

Challenges Wuhu manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Wuhu.

  • Quality systems hidden. Supply-chain buyers look for them first.
  • Specifications in images. Buyers want text tables.
  • No testing shown. Photos and descriptions of test equipment build trust.
  • Inland logistics unexplained. Buyers need routes and terms.

Questions overseas buyers ask Wuhu suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Which quality certifications do you hold?

List quality-system and product certificates with scope.

Can you send PPAP or test documents?

Explain which documents you supply and when.

What is the repeatability and payload?

Put robot and machine specifications in a table on each model page.

Which refrigerants and efficiency standards do you meet?

State them per model and per market.

How do you ship from Wuhu?

Explain river and sea routes, rail freight and terms.

Do you supply batteries or EV components?

Give chemistry, specifications, test reports and certifications per product.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Wuhu manufacturers

  1. Show your quality systems and testing prominently; supply-chain buyers look first.
  2. Give each product family a page with an HTML specification table.
  3. Publish a certifications page by market.
  4. Show production lines, testing and delivered work with your own photos.
  5. Write a logistics page about Yangtze shipping, rail and terms.
  6. Track RFQs by product family and country.
