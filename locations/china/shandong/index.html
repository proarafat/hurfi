Shandong’s manufacturing economy in official figures

The Shandong Provincial Bureau of Statistics’ 2025 bulletin gives the province-wide picture below.

  • Shandong’s GDP was 10,319.75 billion yuan in 2025, up 5.5%, making it the third province and the first in northern China to pass 10 trillion yuan.[1]
  • Value added of industry above designated size grew 7.6% and manufacturing 8.7%; equipment manufacturing grew 11.4% and made up 26.6% of above-designated-size value added.[1]
  • Output of new-energy vehicles was 1.105 million (+50.3%), industrial robots 28,000 sets (+26.6%) and lithium-ion batteries 86.141 million kWh (+103.1%).[1]
  • Jinan, Qingdao and Yantai together had GDP of 4,312.17 billion yuan and contributed 41.4% of the province’s growth.[1]
  • Coastal ports handled 2.19 billion tonnes of cargo (+5.7%) and 48.168 million TEU of containers (+7.0%); goods exports were 2.16 trillion yuan, 48.9% of them mechanical and electrical products.[1]

Shandong’s exporters are heavy-industry and equipment companies with strong ports behind them. Overseas buyers of these products want approvals, specifications and shipping capability on the website before they ask for a quote.

Website and search priorities for Shandong exporters

  1. List approvals, standards and certificates on each product page, with scope and market.
  2. Publish specifications and SDS or test certificates as downloads beside the product text.
  3. Explain shipping from Qingdao, Yantai, Rizhao or other ports, with typical lead times.
  4. Build reference pages for delivered projects you may share.
  5. Plan Korean, Japanese or Russian pages only where you sell.
  6. Measure RFQs by product and country.
