Export websites and Google SEO for Chongqing manufacturers

Chongqing is a municipality and a major inland manufacturing base: cars, motorcycles, laptops, equipment and materials. Its exports travel by rail, river and the New International Land-Sea Trade Corridor. Hurfi helps Chongqing manufacturers present that capacity clearly to overseas buyers.

Hurfi does not have an office or staff in Chongqing. We work with Chongqing manufacturers remotely — WeChat, email and video calls — from our offices in Xi’an (Shaanxi) and Bogura (Bangladesh).

What Chongqing makes, according to official data

Chongqing’s 2025 statistical bulletin and the Liangjiang New Area’s English site give the figures below.

  • Value added of industry above designated size grew 5.9% in 2025: the automobile industry grew 12.6%, motorcycles 18.4%, equipment 8.5% and materials 7.6%.[1]
  • Chongqing produced 2.7877 million vehicles (+9.7%), including 1.2961 million new-energy vehicles (+36.0%), and 6.4731 million motorcycles (+9.1%).[1]
  • Goods trade was 800.68 billion yuan (+12.0%) and exports 558.73 billion yuan (+10.2%); private firms accounted for 48.0% of trade.[1]
  • Vehicle exports (including chassis) were 53.04 billion yuan, up 23.0%, and laptop exports 152.15 billion yuan, 30.2% of China’s laptop exports.[1]

Vehicles and laptops dominate the headline numbers, but behind them sit thousands of parts, motorcycle and materials suppliers. Overseas buyers reach them through distributors, marketplaces and their own searches. A website that shows real models, parts data and export logistics lets a Chongqing supplier be found directly.

Chongqing’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Vehicles and auto parts

Parts and vehicle buyers check part numbers, homologation documents, IATF status and packaging per market.

Motorcycles and new-energy vehicles

Motorcycle and EV buyers compare models, engine or motor data, certifications and dealer support.

Electronics and computers

Electronics suppliers should state processes, test steps and certifications in searchable text.

Equipment and materials

Equipment and materials buyers want specifications, grades, standards and capacity.

Industrial zones and districts in Chongqing

  • Liangjiang New Area became an administrative entity in January 2026 after the former Jiangbei and Yubei districts were merged into it; it covers about 1,360 square kilometres and 3.52 million people.[2]

Liangjiang is the hub for much of Chongqing’s vehicle and electronics industry. A supplier there should show buyers which supply chains it serves, without naming customers it is not allowed to name.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • The New Western Land-Sea Corridor reached 584 ports in 127 countries and regions; Chongqing’s container volume on the corridor rose 30% in 2025.[1]
  • Inland river ports handled 245.16 million tonnes of cargo (+6.9%); container throughput was 2.2503 million TEU, of which 781,500 TEU moved by rail (+23.3%).[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Trade with Belt and Road countries was 369.73 billion yuan (+12.1%), 46.2% of the total; high-tech product exports were 329.16 billion yuan (+4.4%).[1]

Nearly half of Chongqing’s trade is with Belt and Road countries, and rail and the land-sea corridor link it to Southeast and Central Asia and Europe. Language versions and shipping pages for those markets are worth planning early.

Challenges Chongqing manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Chongqing.

  • Parts listed without data. Vehicle and motorcycle buyers want part numbers, materials and fitments.
  • Logistics unexplained. Buyers do not know Chongqing’s rail and corridor routes unless you show them.
  • Wrong-language versions. Russian, Vietnamese or Spanish pages help only if they are accurate and tagged correctly.
  • Generic “group” sites. Large-group sites often bury the product lines that export buyers need.

Questions overseas buyers ask Chongqing suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Which markets are your models certified for?

List certifications and homologation by market for each model.

Can you support dealers with parts and training?

Explain parts supply, manuals and training materials for dealers abroad.

How will the goods reach us — rail, river or sea?

Describe routes and typical transit ranges for the regions you serve.

Can you build to our specification?

Explain OEM options and what can change in design, branding and packaging.

What are your MOQ and payment terms?

Give MOQ ranges and accepted payment terms.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Chongqing manufacturers

  1. Create a page per model or parts family with specifications, certifications by market and downloadable manuals.
  2. Add a dealer and distributor page that explains support, parts, training and territory terms.
  3. Write a logistics page that explains the rail, river and sea routes you use, without promising transit times you cannot control.
  4. Publish part-number tables as text so mechanics and buyers can find them in search.
  5. Use hreflang annotations if you add Spanish, Russian or Arabic pages for dealer markets.
  6. Track dealer inquiries separately from end-customer RFQs.

Questions

Questions from Chongqing manufacturers

Does Hurfi have an office in Chongqing?

No. Hurfi does not have an office or staff in Chongqing. We work with Chongqing manufacturers remotely by WeChat, email and video, from our offices in Xi’an and Bogura.

We export motorcycles through dealers. What should the website do?

Help dealers check you: models, certifications, parts, training and terms. A dealer page with a short application form works well.

Should part numbers be on the website?

Yes, as searchable text where possible. Many buyers search by part number.

Can you build pages in other languages for dealer markets?

Yes, with correct language tags so Google shows the right version to the right users.

How do we explain rail and sea routes?

Describe the routes you actually use and typical transit ranges, and keep it updated.

We are a parts supplier, not a brand. Is SEO still useful?

Yes. Buyers search for part types and specifications; detailed pages can be found for those searches.

Do you run Google Ads?

Yes, as part of digital marketing, for chosen models and countries.

Can you guarantee results?

No. We plan for qualified inquiries and report honestly from your own accounts.
