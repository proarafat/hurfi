Export websites and Google SEO for Nantong manufacturers

Nantong, on the north bank of the Yangtze opposite Shanghai, builds ships and offshore equipment, spins chemical fibre, weaves cloth, sews garments and makes transformers, optical fibre and chips. Its exports rose 17% in 2025. Hurfi helps Nantong makers win the buyers and engineers who compare suppliers online.

What Nantong makes, according to official data

The figures below come from Nantong’s 2025 statistical bulletin.

  • Nantong’s GDP was 1,280.15 billion yuan in 2025, up 5.3%, and value added of industry above designated size grew 7.0%.[1]
  • Equipment manufacturing grew 11.6%; within it, rail, ship, aerospace and other transport equipment grew 26.5%.[1]
  • The city completed ships of 4.287 million deadweight tonnes and made 6.501 million tonnes of chemical fibre, 35.75 billion integrated circuits and 66.048 million kilometres of optical fibre.[1]
  • Goods trade was 440.73 billion yuan (+12.3%) and exports 303.59 billion yuan (+17.0%).[1]
  • Trade with Belt and Road countries made up 51.3% of the total.[1]
  • High-tech manufacturing grew 10.6% and made up 16.2% of above-designated-size industry; transformer output rose 22.8% to 58.241 million kVA.[1]

Nantong sells two very different things abroad: large engineered equipment bought on approvals and references, and textiles and fibre bought on samples, specifications and price. A website that serves both needs separate paths — and real detail on each. Nantong’s growth in 2025 came from Europe, ASEAN and Africa, so pages should be written for buyers in those markets as well as for the United States.

Nantong’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Ships, offshore and marine equipment

Marine buyers check classification-society approvals, delivered projects and yard capability. Give dates and scope for every reference.

Textiles and garments

Textile buyers compare fibre content, weights, finishes, test standards and MOQs per collection.

Chemical fibre and materials

Fibre buyers look for grades, deniers, specifications and packaging, with test data per product.

Transformers and electrical equipment

Electrical buyers ask for ratings, standards, type tests and project references.

Chips, optical fibre and electronics

Electronics buyers want part numbers, parameters and quality systems in searchable text.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Nantong’s port handled 290.41 million tonnes of cargo in 2025 and 2.47 million TEU of containers (−9.2%).[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Exports to the EU rose 20.7% to 47.18 billion yuan, to ASEAN 20.9% to 51.08 billion and to Africa 15.8% to 25.36 billion.[1]
  • Exports to the United States fell 19.3% to 27.60 billion yuan.[1]

Nantong’s 2025 growth came from ASEAN, the EU and Africa while US exports fell. Pages for those markets need the right certifications, terms and references — not just translated text.

Challenges Nantong manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Nantong.

  • Projects without proof. Equipment buyers expect dated references and approvals.
  • Textile pages without data. Fibre content, weight and test standards are often missing.
  • One message for every buyer. Shipowners, brands and distributors want different things.
  • Market pages without substance. Translation alone does not convince buyers in new markets.

Questions overseas buyers ask Nantong suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Which classification societies have approved your work?

List approvals with scope and products, and link them from each product page.

Can we see projects you have delivered?

Publish references you are allowed to show, with year, scope and photos.

What is the fibre content and test standard?

Put composition, weights and test standards on each fabric or fibre page.

What MOQ and lead time apply?

State realistic MOQs and lead times per product family.

How do you ship from Nantong?

Explain the river and sea ports you use, packing and typical terms.

Can you build to our class or project specification?

Explain how you work to customer specifications and which documents you deliver with each order.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Nantong manufacturers

  1. Split the site by buyer: project equipment on one path, textiles and fibre on another.
  2. Build reference pages for delivered ships, equipment or textile programmes you can show.
  3. Publish specifications and test standards in text on every product page; offer PDFs beside them.
  4. Write a logistics page about your port, packing and terms.
  5. Add market pages only where you have customers, certifications and support.
  6. Track RFQs by product line and country so effort goes where buyers respond.
  7. Publish an approvals page grouped by market and by classification society, linked from each product.
