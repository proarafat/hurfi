Export websites and Google SEO for Fuzhou manufacturers

Fuzhou, Fujian’s capital, is a base for textiles and chemical fibre, steel, vehicles, displays, electronics and equipment. Its factories grew in 2025, but its exports fell sharply. Hurfi helps Fuzhou makers rebuild overseas demand with websites that show buyers exactly what they make.

Hurfi does not have an office or staff in Fuzhou. We work with Fuzhou manufacturers remotely — WeChat, email and video calls — from our offices in Xi’an (Shaanxi) and Bogura (Bangladesh).

What Fuzhou makes, according to official data

The figures below come from Fuzhou’s 2025 statistical bulletin, published by the Fuzhou Statistics Bureau.

  • Fuzhou’s GDP was 1,511.232 billion yuan in 2025, up 5.6%; industrial value added was 340.746 billion yuan (+6.9%), and above-designated-size industry grew 7.2%.[1]
  • Automobile manufacturing grew 20.1%, chemicals 30.7%, electronics 24.9% and steel 18.8%; equipment manufacturing grew 21.6% and high-tech manufacturing 14.3%.[1]
  • The city made 119,900 cars (+41.6%), 3.5754 million tonnes of chemical fibre and 22.4783 million displays (−15.6%); yarn output fell 29.5%.[1]
  • Goods trade fell 24.0% to 266.43 billion yuan, and exports fell 32.0% to 174.73 billion yuan.[1]
  • Exports to the United States fell 34.5%, to the EU 12.1% and to ASEAN 60.2%.[1]
  • Garment output was 54.4095 million pieces and crude steel 15.609 million tonnes.[1]

2025 was a difficult export year for Fuzhou even as its factories produced more. That makes winning new direct buyers more important. Buyers who find a Fuzhou supplier online need detail, proof and a clear route to a quote — and a supplier that is honest about what it does well. Exports to the EU and Japan fell far less than exports to the US and ASEAN, which gives a starting point for planning.

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Displays and electronics

Equipment and machinery

Equipment buyers compare specifications, service and references, one family at a time.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Fuzhou’s port handled 244.9645 million tonnes of cargo in 2025 (−2.7%) and 3.5956 million TEU of containers (+0.2%).[1]
  • Foreign-trade cargo at the port rose 6.8% to 75.8677 million tonnes, even as total cargo fell.[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Exports to the United States were 24.275 billion yuan (−34.5%), to the EU 29.434 billion (−12.1%) and to ASEAN 33.636 billion (−60.2%).[1]
  • Exports to Japan were 8.644 billion yuan (−12.9%); 868 new foreign-invested firms were set up (+19.7%).[1]

Fuzhou’s exports fell in every major market in 2025, ASEAN most of all. Exporters rebuilding demand should pick a few markets, prepare the certifications and terms those buyers need, and build pages for them.

Challenges Fuzhou manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Fuzhou.

  • Relying on old channels. When demand falls, direct buyers matter more.
  • Products without data. Specifications and standards are often missing.
  • No market focus. Pages written for everyone convince no one.
  • Stock photos. Buyers trust real factory images.

Questions overseas buyers ask Fuzhou suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Can you send specifications and test reports?

Put specifications on each product page and say which reports are available.

Which grade and standard does this steel meet?

Give grades, standards and the certificates you supply.

Can you make OEM products?

Explain OEM, MOQ and design protection on a separate page.

What capacity and lead time apply?

Give realistic ranges per product family.

How do you ship from Fuzhou?

Name the port and terms you use and typical transit times.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Fuzhou manufacturers

  1. Show what you make and to which standards on the first screen.
  2. Give each product family a page with specifications in HTML.
  3. Publish a certifications page by market.
  4. Write market pages for the regions you are targeting next, with real terms and certifications.
  5. Use real factory and product photos with short captions.
  6. Track RFQs by product line and country to see which new markets respond.
