Export websites and SEO for Ningbo manufacturers

Ningbo sits on one of the world’s busiest ports and has more national “single champion” manufacturers than any other Chinese city. Many of them make one component very well. Hurfi helps Ningbo manufacturers show that specialism to buyers abroad, and be found when those buyers search.

Hurfi does not have an office or staff in Ningbo. We work with Ningbo manufacturers remotely — WeChat, email and video calls — from our offices in Xi’an (Shaanxi) and Bogura (Bangladesh).

What Ningbo makes, according to official data

Ningbo’s 2025 statistical bulletin sets out industrial growth, export structure and port activity. We read the full text as republished by CCPIT Ningbo, a government-affiliated trade body.

  • Value added of industrial enterprises above designated size grew 5.3% in 2025. Equipment manufacturing value added was 321.1 billion yuan, up 5.9%, or 62.3% of the total; high-tech manufacturing grew 11.9%.[1]
  • Output of electronic components rose 75.0%, industrial robots 46.4% and integrated circuits 10.6%.[1]
  • The bulletin counts 119 national manufacturing single-champion enterprises in Ningbo, the most of any Chinese city, and 511 specialised “little giant” firms.[1]
  • Goods exports reached 980.8 billion yuan, up 3.7%, and mechanical and electrical products made up 58.2% of exports.[1]
  • Ningbo-Zhoushan Port handled 1.43 billion tonnes of cargo, first in the world for the 17th year, and 43.872 million TEU of containers, up 11.6% and third in the world.[1]

A single-champion company leads its national market in one narrow product. That is an export story, but only if the website tells it: what exactly you make, for which applications, at what volume and quality. Ningbo’s export mix is mostly mechanical and electrical, so buyers tend to be engineers and purchasing teams comparing specifications.

Ningbo’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Precision components and moulds

Component and mould suppliers win on tolerances, materials, tooling life and inspection. Show those numbers and the equipment behind them.

Machinery and equipment

Equipment is the largest part of Ningbo industry. Machine pages need specifications, video, spare-parts policy and service arrangements abroad.

Electrical products and appliances

Electrical buyers check certificates by market and model, voltage options and packaging for export.

Robotics and electronic components

With robots and components growing fast, these suppliers should explain integration, controller compatibility and test data.

Industrial zones and districts in Ningbo

  • Ningbo Economic and Technological Development Zone in Beilun is described by the Zhejiang government as a petrochemical and steel base built on Beilun Port, with Geely Automobile as the flagship of its automobile industry.[4]
  • The same source says the zone’s plastic injection-moulding machine base is known as the “Capital of Plastic Injection Molding Machine in China”, and its mould-making base as “Die-casting Mold Town in China”.[4]

Beilun’s injection-moulding machinery and die-casting moulds are good examples of Ningbo’s narrow, deep specialisms — exactly the kind of capability that needs specific product and process pages.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • At the end of 2025 Ningbo-Zhoushan Port had 309 container routes, 151 of them ocean trunk routes.[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Ningbo traded directly with 234 countries and regions. Trade with Belt and Road partner countries was 741.3 billion yuan (+8.8%), with the EU 251.1 billion yuan (+5.1%) and with ASEAN 216.2 billion yuan (+15.2%).[1]
  • Private enterprises accounted for 77.9% of the city’s trade.[1]

With trade partners in 234 countries and regions, Ningbo exporters sell everywhere, but the bulletin shows the EU and ASEAN as large partners and ASEAN growing fastest. Typical products include machinery, moulds, electrical products, electronic components and industrial robots. Specification pages written for engineers in both regions — metric units, standards named, export packaging explained — fit this mix.

Challenges Ningbo manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Ningbo.

  • Specialists who look like generalists. Many single-champion firms hide their specialism behind a long, generic product list.
  • Dependence on a few large customers. Companies that grew through long-term OEM contracts often have little online presence for new buyers to find.
  • Drawings and confidentiality. Mould and component makers cannot show customer parts, so they need process and capability proof instead.
  • Logistics questions unanswered. Buyers want packaging, lead-time and trade-term information that is rarely on the site.

Questions overseas buyers ask Ningbo suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

What exactly is your specialism?

Say it in one sentence at the top of the home page, then prove it with volumes, applications and tolerances.

Can you ship from Ningbo on our schedule?

Explain lead times, packaging and the Incoterms you normally quote, without promising dates you cannot keep.

Do you make the tooling yourselves?

Describe your toolroom or mould shop, its equipment and who owns the tooling after it is paid for.

What inspection reports come with each shipment?

List the reports — dimensional, material, functional — and show an anonymised sample.

Can you support a long product life?

Explain how long you keep parts, drawings and tools available, and how you handle change notices.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Ningbo manufacturers

  1. Put your specialism in the page title and first heading of the home page, in the buyer’s words — for example “precision die-cast aluminium housings” rather than “hardware products”.
  2. Build one page per product family with a specification table, materials, finishes and the industries it serves.
  3. Add a manufacturing page that lists equipment, toolroom capability and inspection instruments, with photos you own.
  4. Make export logistics easy to read: packaging, lead-time ranges, the port you ship from and the trade terms you quote.
  5. List the product page URLs in an XML sitemap. Google notes sitemaps help larger sites and new sites get crawled efficiently.
  6. Give the RFQ form a drawing upload and ask for annual volume, so your team can quote the right process.

Questions

Questions from Ningbo manufacturers

Does Hurfi have an office in Ningbo?

No. We have no office or staff in Ningbo. We work with Ningbo manufacturers remotely by WeChat, email and video, from Xi’an and Bogura.

We are a single-champion company. Can we say that on our website?

Yes, if you hold the designation. State the product it covers and the year, and link to the official list if one is published. Do not extend it to products it does not cover.

Most of our business is with a few large customers. Why invest in a website?

To reduce that dependence. New buyers search by product and specification; a website that ranks for those searches brings inquiries you would not otherwise get.

Should we show prices?

For custom parts, usually not. Show what drives the price — material, volume, tolerance, finish — and invite an RFQ with drawings.

How do we handle many product variants?

Use one page per product family with a variant table, rather than hundreds of near-identical pages. Thin, repeated pages can hurt more than help.

Do you work with mould and tooling makers?

Yes. Tooling buyers want capacity, steel grades, tool life, trial process and lead time. We help you present those clearly.

Can you help us reach buyers in specific countries?

Yes, through English pages written for those markets, search targeting and, where it fits, paid campaigns aimed at those countries.

Can you guarantee a number of inquiries?

No. We plan for qualified inquiries and report them honestly from your own accounts, but we do not promise a number.
