What Wenzhou makes, according to official data

Wenzhou’s 2025 statistical bulletin and the Zhejiang government’s English portal give the figures below.

  • Wenzhou had 9,372 industrial enterprises above designated size in 2025; their value added was 192.00 billion yuan, up 10.3%, and export delivery value was 116.27 billion yuan, up 9.8%.[1]
  • 9,221 private industrial firms above designated size produced 90.7% of that value added, and private firms made 96.1% of the city’s exports.[1]
  • Goods trade passed 300 billion yuan for the first time, at 311.65 billion yuan (+6.1%); exports were 276.19 billion yuan (+9.6%), with mechanical and electrical products 151.12 billion yuan (+11.6%).[1]
  • Footwear exports were 26.31 billion yuan (−7.3%) and clothing 14.31 billion yuan (−5.5%), while high-tech products grew 32.9%.[1]
  • The provincial portal names five traditional industries — electrical appliances, footwear, clothing, auto and motorcycle parts, and pumps and valves — and says Wenzhou is home to 46 national production bases.[2]

Two trends sit side by side: electrical and mechanical exports are growing, while shoes and clothing are under pressure. Factories in both groups sell to buyers who compare many Wenzhou suppliers at once. The ones that win are the ones whose websites make specifications, certifications and order terms easy to check.

Wenzhou’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Low-voltage electrical products

Breaker, contactor and switchgear buyers filter by rating, standard and certification mark per market. Publish those per series.

Pumps and valves

Pump and valve buyers ask for materials, pressure classes, sizes, end connections and test certificates. One page per series answers most of them.

Auto and motorcycle parts

Parts buyers check cross-reference numbers, materials, IATF status and packaging. Searchable part data brings better RFQs.

Footwear and apparel

Brand buyers look for sampling speed, materials, compliance tests and capacity, and whether you design or only produce.

Industrial zones and districts in Wenzhou

  • At the end of 2025 Wenzhou had one national high-tech zone and eight provincial-level high-tech zones.[1]
  • The provincial portal says Wenzhou is home to 46 national production bases, including the “Capital of Electric Appliances in China” and the “Capital of Shoes in China”.[2]

Many Wenzhou buyers already know the city’s reputation for electrical products and shoes. What they do not know is which of thousands of suppliers is right for them. Specific, checkable product pages are the way to stand out from the base.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Wenzhou’s ports handled 1.621 million TEU of containers in 2025 (+11.5%), and the airport 129,000 tonnes of cargo and mail (−0.5%).[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • Exports to Belt and Road countries grew 13.4%, to ASEAN 16.8% and to the Middle East 18.0%.[1]
  • 13,820 firms had import or export business; 807 exported more than 10 million US dollars and 89 more than 50 million.[1]

Growth is fastest in ASEAN, the Middle East and other Belt and Road markets. If those are your targets, plan pages, certifications and possibly languages for them, rather than only for Europe and North America.

Challenges Wenzhou manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Wenzhou.

  • Look-alike sites. Many suppliers use the same templates and stock photos, so buyers cannot tell them apart.
  • Certificates hidden or vague. Buyers of electrical products filter on marks per market; if they cannot find them, they move on.
  • No language plan. Machine-translated pages for every language rarely help; a few well-made versions for real markets do.
  • Family-run sales. Inquiries reach one person’s phone and get lost; a simple RFQ form and process fixes that.

Questions overseas buyers ask Wenzhou suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Which certification marks do these products carry?

List CE, UL, CB or other marks per product series, with the market each applies to.

What is your MOQ per model or colour?

Give MOQ ranges and say when mixed orders are possible.

Can you put our brand on it?

Explain OEM labelling, packaging and any minimums for private label.

How do you test before shipping?

Describe routine tests, inspection rates and the reports you can send.

Which ports do you ship from, and how fast?

State the usual port, typical lead times and the Incoterms you quote.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Wenzhou manufacturers

  1. Organise products by series with model tables (ratings, sizes, materials) so buyers can compare in seconds.
  2. Put certificates on the product pages they cover, with the issuing body and scope written out.
  3. Add an OEM and private-label page that explains what can be branded, minimums and approval steps.
  4. Write a short “how we test” page per product group, with photos of the actual test equipment.
  5. Use Google’s guidance on multi-regional sites before adding Spanish, Arabic or Russian pages for the markets you sell to.
  6. Measure which product series bring RFQs, and expand those pages first.
