Export websites and Google SEO for manufacturers in Hefei

Hefei has grown into a major base for electric vehicles, electronics and displays. Suppliers in those chains increasingly sell abroad, and overseas buyers look them up online first. Hurfi helps Hefei manufacturers prepare for that, working remotely from Xi’an and Bogura.

Hurfi has no office or staff in Hefei. We work with Hefei manufacturers remotely over WeChat, email and video calls, from our offices in Xi’an (Shaanxi) and Bogura (Bangladesh).

What Hefei makes, according to official data

Recent figures released through the Hefei Statistics Bureau show which industries are driving the city’s growth. We use them to choose the industry pages and search topics that matter for a Hefei supplier.

  • Citing the Hefei Statistics Bureau, Anhui Economic News reported that Hefei’s above-designated-size industrial value added grew 17.6% in 2025, led by computer, communications and other electronic equipment manufacturing (up 60.6%) and automobile manufacturing (up 11.1%), described as the city’s two pillar industries.[1]
  • The same report gives Hefei’s 2025 output of new energy vehicles as 1.371 million, 8.3% of the national total, and microcomputer output as 27.72 million units.[1]
  • The report refers to the city’s “芯屏汽合” identity — chips, displays and automobiles — as its industrial landmark.[1]

What this means for a Hefei manufacturer’s website

EV supply chain

Suppliers to vehicle makers need clear statements of quality system, part specifications and the approvals actually held for each market.

Electronics and semiconductors

Component and device makers need searchable datasheets and compliance documents, not only a PDF catalog.

Batteries and energy

Battery and storage suppliers face early questions on test data, certificates and dangerous-goods shipping. The website should answer them.

Displays and optics

Display and optical component suppliers need specification pages with tolerances and test data engineers can compare.

Where we would usually start

For a Hefei manufacturer selling abroad, the first pages and search topics usually follow the city’s industrial mix:

  • Product pages for EV, battery and display-related components with the ratings and test data buyers ask for first.
  • Certification and quality pages that answer automotive buyers’ questions about approval processes.
  • Content that explains how you support overseas customers after delivery: documents, samples, technical contact.
  • Search work aimed at component and specification terms in the vehicle, battery, display and semiconductor supply chains.
