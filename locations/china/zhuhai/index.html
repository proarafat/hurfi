What Zhuhai makes, according to official data

Zhuhai’s 2025 statistical bulletin, published on the city government portal, gives the figures below.

  • Zhuhai’s GDP was 457.310 billion yuan in 2025, up 2.7%, and value added of industry above designated size grew 4.1%.[1]
  • Advanced manufacturing made up 58.4% of above-designated-size industrial value added (+6.0%), equipment manufacturing 38.5% (+6.7%) and high-tech manufacturing 30.9% (+2.5%).[1]
  • Zhuhai produced 43,702 industrial robot sets (+35.7%); output of lithium-ion batteries rose 23.3%, solar cells 83.4% and optical instruments 21.7%.[1]
  • Foreign trade was 335.433 billion yuan (+3.4%), with exports of 228.684 billion yuan (−0.4%).[1]
  • The ports handled 120.91 million tonnes of cargo (−3.8%) and 1.37 million TEU of containers (+6.7%).[1]

Zhuhai’s industry is compact and technical: appliances, robots, printers, batteries and instruments. Buyers of these products compare specifications, certifications and service across many suppliers. Clear English product pages, with the data in text, are often what gets a Zhuhai supplier onto the shortlist.

Zhuhai’s main industries and what buyers compare

The clusters below follow the official figures above. Each links to our page for that industry.

Electrical appliances

Appliance buyers check models, energy ratings, voltages and certification marks per market. Publish them per model.

Robots and automation

Robot buyers compare payload, reach, repeatability and integration support.

Electronics and printers

Electronics buyers look for parameters, compatibility, standards and OEM options in searchable text.

Batteries and solar

Battery and PV buyers ask for test reports, certifications and capacity before price.

Medical and optical equipment

Medical and optical buyers check registrations, standards and intended use per market.

Industrial zones and districts in Zhuhai

  • Xiangzhou, Jinwan and Doumen districts had GDP of 304.770, 96.461 and 56.079 billion yuan in 2025; industrial value added grew 0.4% in Xiangzhou, 6.1% in Jinwan and 13.5% in Doumen.[1]

Industrial growth was strongest in Jinwan and Doumen, Zhuhai’s western districts. Wherever you are, buyers want to see real production and real testing.

Ports, logistics and trade fairs

  • Zhuhai’s ports had 144 berths at the end of 2025 and handled 1.37 million TEU of containers (+6.7%).[1]

Buyers comparing suppliers ask how goods will reach them, how long it takes and on what trade terms. Shipping ports, typical lead times and Incoterms on your website answer that before it is asked.

Export profile, typical products and buyer markets

  • General trade made up 164.764 billion yuan of exports and processing trade 56.702 billion yuan; trade with Belt and Road countries was 132.175 billion yuan (+0.4%).[1]

Zhuhai’s bulletin lists ASEAN, the EU, Hong Kong (China), the US and India among its main export markets. A site that handles certifications per market makes it easier to sell across all of them.

Challenges Zhuhai manufacturers face online

These are common problems on manufacturers’ export websites, tied to the industry mix above. They are general observations, not statistics about Zhuhai.

  • Certification marks unclear. Appliance and electronics buyers filter by them.
  • OEM and own-brand mixed. Brand buyers and distributors need different pages.
  • Thin model pages. One photo and a line of text does not win RFQs.
  • No measurement. Without tracking, nobody knows which models attract buyers.

Questions overseas buyers ask Zhuhai suppliers

These are typical questions in a first exchange with an overseas buyer, and what your website can show so the answer is already there.

Which certification marks does this model carry for our market?

List them per model and market.

Is this compatible with our system?

Publish compatibility and interface information per product.

Can you supply under our brand?

Explain OEM and ODM options, minimums and approval steps.

Which test reports can you send?

Name the reports and the labs that issued them.

How quickly can you ship from Zhuhai?

State the port or route, typical lead times and terms.

A website, SEO and RFQ playbook for Zhuhai manufacturers

  1. Give each model a page with specifications, compatibility and certification marks per market.
  2. Separate OEM/ODM services from your own-brand products so both buyers find the right pages.
  3. Publish a quality and testing page with the test equipment, standards and reports you can share.
  4. Write application pages that explain where each product is used and which specifications matter.
  5. Use Google’s product structured-data guidance where it suits your product pages.
  6. Measure RFQs by model and country, and develop the pages that work.
