Hunan’s manufacturing economy in official figures

The Hunan Provincial Bureau of Statistics’ 2025 bulletin gives the figures below.

  • Hunan’s GDP was 5,530.87 billion yuan in 2025, up 4.8%; value added of industry above designated size grew 6.2%.[1]
  • Equipment manufacturing grew 8.6% and made up 33.9% of above-designated-size value added; high-tech manufacturing grew 11.2%.[1]
  • The province made 44,000 units of concrete machinery (+39.6%) and 1.626 million cars (+30.8%).[1]
  • Exports were 320.76 billion yuan (−4.1%); mechanical and electrical exports rose 2.0% to 187.47 billion yuan.[1]
  • Exports to ASEAN rose 5.5% to 63.28 billion yuan and to the EU 0.4% to 32.72 billion, while exports to the United States fell 29.2% to 22.27 billion.[1]
  • Private firms made 64.5% of above-designated-size industrial value added; processing-trade exports rose 21.1% to 54.09 billion yuan while general-trade exports fell 7.8%.[1]

Hunan’s machinery and rail makers sell big, engineered products, often into infrastructure projects in emerging markets. Buyers compare specifications, service and references. A website that shows them clearly supports every tender and dealer conversation. Most of its industry is private, and many of these firms are now looking for direct overseas buyers.

More Hunan manufacturing cities, from their 2025 bulletins

These cities do not have a full guide yet. The figures come from each city’s 2025 statistical bulletin, listed in the sources below.

Zhuzhou

  • Zhuzhou’s GDP was 406.35 billion yuan in 2025 (+5.6%); value added of industry above designated size grew 9.6%.[2]
  • Equipment manufacturing made up 43.6% of above-designated-size value added and grew 10.9%.[2]
  • Exports were 19.57 billion yuan, up 3.9%.[2]
  • Revenue of above-designated-size industry was 311.75 billion yuan (+6.2%).[2]

Equipment makes up more than two-fifths of Zhuzhou’s industry. Its suppliers should show standards, approvals and delivered projects — the evidence engineering buyers ask for first. Related: Industrial Machinery & CNC · Zhuzhou in the China directory.

Website and search priorities for Hunan exporters

  1. Give each machine family a page with specifications, applications and service.
  2. Publish a service and spare-parts page for buyers abroad.
  3. Build reference pages for projects you are allowed to show.
  4. Separate pages for dealers and for end users.
  5. Plan market pages for ASEAN and other growing regions where you have support.
  6. Track RFQs by machine family and country.
