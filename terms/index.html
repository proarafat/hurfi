Using the site

You can browse Hurfi and send an inquiry. Please send accurate information about your business.

Do not send content you do not have the right to share.

An inquiry is not a project yet

Sending the form, a WeChat message, or an email does not start the work.

Hurfi will reply with questions or a proposal. Nothing is booked until both sides agree the next step in writing.

The written scope

A project begins when Hurfi and you agree the scope in writing. That written scope is the list of work both sides are counting on.

The list can include a website, search, campaigns, content, or consulting. Only the items written down are part of the project.

Changes to the scope

If you want work that is not on that list, Hurfi and you agree the extra work in writing before it starts.

A change can also change the time and the payment. The new writing replaces the old list for the items it names.

Payment

  • Hurfi tells you the payment in the written proposal.
  • An advance, when there is one, follows the Refund & Return Policy unless a later written proposal says otherwise.
  • After the agreed payment, the finished deliverables in the scope are yours to use for your business.

What Hurfi does not promise

Hurfi does not promise a specific search ranking, a fixed number of inquiries, or a sales result. The work is the website, search, campaigns, and content described in the scope.

Screenshots, examples, and past projects on this site show earlier work. They are not a promise that your project will look the same or produce the same result.

Your part

  • You provide the materials and feedback the scope needs, in reasonable time. Delays on your side move the schedule.
  • You make sure Hurfi may use the logos, photos, product facts, and accounts you share.
  • If the scope needs access to a domain, a site, or an ad account, you provide that access or name a person who can.

The finished work

Hurfi may show the finished work in its portfolio unless you say in writing that it should stay private.

Hurfi keeps the right to reuse its own methods, layouts that were not made only for you, and general know-how.

Stopping early

You can ask to stop. Hurfi will say how much of the written scope is already complete.

  • If that share is still under 60%, the advance follows the Refund & Return Policy.
  • Once 60% of the agreed scope is complete, the advance is not returned, and the work already done stays with you.

If two documents disagree

If these terms and a written proposal do not match, the written proposal applies to that project.
