Privacy Policy

How Hurfi handles the information you share.

Updated 16 October 2026

Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Refund & Return Policy

Who this covers

Hurfi collects information when you write to us, book a conversation, or send the inquiry form on this site.

This page covers visitors to this site and the people who contact Hurfi about a project.

Hurfi works from Bogura in Bangladesh and Xi'an in China. Information you send may be read by the team in both places.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send, and what this browser stores for the language switch.

  • Your name, so we know who to reply to.
  • A business email, and a WhatsApp number or WeChat ID if you choose to share one.
  • Your company name, the service you are asking about, your budget range, and the project details you write.
  • Messages you later send by email, WeChat, or WhatsApp about the same project.
  • Your language choice, stored in this browser only, so the site opens in the same language next time.

What this site does not ask for

  • This site does not ask for an identity number, a bank password, or a card number.
  • Hurfi does not sell personal information.
  • Hurfi does not buy contact lists. The details we hold are the ones you send us.

Why we use it

We use this information to reply to you, prepare a proposal, and do the work you ask Hurfi to do.

  • We also use it to tell you how much of an agreed scope is complete, if you ask about a payment.
  • We do not use your details to send unrelated promotions.

Where the team reads it

If you are in China, this notice tells you that your information may leave mainland China so the team in Bangladesh can work on your project.

The inquiry form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which receives what you type and emails it to Hurfi’s business mailboxes (yasir@hurfi.com and support@hurfi.com).

After that, only the people at Hurfi who are replying to you or doing the project read it.

Services this site uses

Some outside services handle data so this site can work. Each one has its own privacy policy.

  • Netlify hosts the site. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the site for speed and security, and also sees the request details of each page load.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives the inquiry form and forwards it to Hurfi by email. It may show a captcha to check that a person sent the form.
  • Google Fonts serves the site’s fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers when a page loads.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and project details while we are working together or still in conversation. When they are no longer needed, we delete them.

A project file can stay while you are still a client, or while Hurfi needs it to answer a question about that project.

Your choices

You can ask Hurfi to show you the information we hold, correct it, or delete it. You can also tell us to stop using it.

  • If you are in China, you can also ask for an explanation of these rules, and you can withdraw a choice you made earlier.
  • Asking us to delete a project file does not cancel a written scope. The project terms stay in that document.

Email yasir@hurfi.com and we will respond.

If this page changes

When this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.

The rules for a project already agreed in writing stay the ones in that written proposal.
