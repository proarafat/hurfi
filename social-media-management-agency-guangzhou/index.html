Questions about social media management in Guangzhou

What does a social media management agency in Guangzhou provide?

It plans, creates, publishes and manages content across your social accounts: account management, content calendars, graphic design, promotional posts, Facebook and Instagram management, engagement planning and performance monitoring.

How much does social media management cost in Guangzhou?

It depends on the number of platforms, monthly content volume, design and video needs and account management responsibilities. Advertising spend is separate from management fees. We prepare a proposal for your requirements.

What is included in professional social media management?

Account optimisation, audience research, content planning, graphic design, caption writing, scheduling, publishing and performance monitoring, sometimes with short-form video and advertising support. The deliverables depend on the agreed scope.

Can social media help Guangzhou manufacturers attract international buyers?

It helps manufacturers show their product range, production capabilities and company information to international audiences, which supports brand awareness and buyer research. Qualified enquiries also depend on targeting, product demand and your overall marketing.

Which social media platforms should Guangzhou businesses use?

For international audiences, consider Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube. For mainland China, WeChat, Douyin, Xiaohongshu or Weibo. Choose by customer behaviour and what your team can sustain.

Does Hurfi provide Facebook and Instagram management in Guangzhou?

Yes, for eligible international markets: account management, content planning, branded graphics, promotional posts and performance monitoring, with advertising available subject to platform eligibility.

Can Hurfi create social media content for B2B companies?

Yes: product introductions, manufacturing updates, company announcements, industry information, promotional graphics and short videos for manufacturers, exporters, industrial suppliers and other B2B companies.

How often should a business post on social media?

It depends on the platform, audience and the content you can produce. Relevant, consistent posts beat a large number of weak ones, and a monthly calendar keeps the schedule manageable.

Does social media management include paid advertising?

Not necessarily. Organic management covers content and account activity; paid advertising uses a campaign budget to reach selected audiences. Hurfi offers both, scoped separately.

Does Hurfi have an office in Guangzhou?

No. We serve businesses across Guangzhou, including Tianhe, Huangpu, Panyu, Baiyun and Nansha, remotely through online meetings, WeChat and email. Our offices are in Xi’an, China, and Bogura, Bangladesh.

How can I start working with Hurfi on social media?

Book a free 30-minute social media strategy call. Share your website, current social accounts, target audience and content needs, and we will prepare a proposal.
